# Privacy Policy
We, [WPManageNinja](<https://wpmanageninja.com/>) (the parent company of FluentBoards), are committed to safeguarding your data, ensuring a seamless experience, and upholding the highest privacy and security standards. By being transparent about our practices and principles, we aim to build a strong and trustworthy partnership with you. 
To learn more about the WPManageNinja privacy policy, [click here](<https://wpmanageninja.com/privacy/>).
## What Data is Being Collected by FluentBoards?
FluentBoards (**both the website and plugin**) doesn’t gather your data for any surveillance purposes. Instead, we collect data to enhance your browsing and purchasing experience. Here’s the information we collect:
### When You Visit fluentboards.com 
When you visit the FluentBoards website we collect the following information – 
  * **Device information**

When you visit the Site, we gather specific device-related data automatically. This includes details about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and certain cookies on your device. 
Additionally, when you navigate the Site, we collect information about the particular web pages or products you view, the websites or search terms that brought you here, and how you engage with the Site. We term this automatically-gathered data as ‘Device Information’.
  * **Order information**

When you buy something on the site, we collect specific details from you: your name, billing address, shipping address, payment info (like your PayPal email), and email address. We call this ‘Order Information.’ (**Please note that we don’t store credit card information**)
  * **Personal information**

When we mention personal information in this Privacy Policy, we mean both ‘device information’ and ‘order information’.
### When You Use FluentBoards Plugin
The information that we collect when you install FluentBoards on your WordPress website and use it to meet all your scheduling and appointment booking needs includes: 
  * **Product user’s information**

When product users (website admins) install the FluentBoards plugin on their WordPress website, they consciously agree to the terms or conditions regarding specific data accessible to third parties via their developer API. Note that, if the product users turned off the Auto Update or Auto Sync feature, still some data (previously cached) may be stored in the product user’s website database. However, FluentBoards is not responsible for this cached data. 
  * **End user’s information**

When end-users book an appointment using FluentBoards, they consciously agree to the terms or conditions regarding specific data accessible to third parties via their developer API. However, FluentBoards only forwards the information consciously provided by the end users to the concerned authority and never fetches/processes/stores any user data that is not permitted by users to be shared publicly.
## How does FluentBoards Collect Your Data?
We gather ‘device information’ through these methods:
  * **Cookies:** These are data files placed on your device, often with a unique identifier. To learn more about cookies and how to disable them, you can visit <http://www.allaboutcookies.org>

  * **Log files:** These keep track of actions on the Site, including your IP address, browser type, Internet service provider, pages you visit, and time of your visit.

  * **‘Google Analytics,’ ‘events,’ and ‘pixels’:** These tools record traffic data and your interactions with the site.”

Additionally, when you purchase or attempt to purchase the plugin, we collect certain information from you, including your—
  * Name
  * IP address
  * Billing address
  * Shipping address
  * Email address
  * Payment information

## How do FluentBoards Utilize the Collected Information?
FluentBoards utilizes Users’ personal information for the following purposes:
  * **Personalizing user experience:** We may use this information to tailor the user experience and understand how our Users collectively engage with our services and resources on the Site.

  * **Processing transactions:** When Users place orders, we use the provided information solely to fulfill those orders. **We don’t share this information with external parties** unless necessary for providing the service.

  * **Administering site features:** This information helps us manage site features like content, promotions, surveys, or other activities.

  * **Sending relevant information:** We send Users information they’ve agreed to receive on topics we believe will interest them.

  * **Periodic emails:** For order-related matters, we use the email address provided by Users to send order updates, respond to inquiries, and fulfill other requests. If Users opt into our mailing list, they may receive emails containing company news, updates, product or service information, and more. 

  * **Users can unsubscribe from these emails** at any time using the instructions provided at the bottom of each email or by contacting us through our Site.

## How We Protect Your Information?
We employ suitable methods for gathering, storing, and processing data, along with security measures, to safeguard against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of your personal information, username, password, transaction details, and data stored on our Site. 
The exchange of sensitive and private information between the Site and Users occurs through an **SSL-secured communication channel**. This data is encrypted and further protected with digital signatures.
Here are some other measures that we take to keep your data safe and secure—

At FluentBoards, we fully comply with [GDPR regulations](<https://gdpr-info.eu/>) and uphold all standard practices when collecting and using your data. We are committed to ensuring transparency in how we collect, store, and utilize the data of our customers and Users (you). Since our company’s inception, we have consistently upheld rigorous data protection and privacy principles

Regarding email communications, FluentBoards adheres to all the [CAN-SPAM Act](<https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business>) requirements. ### Third-party Data Sharing
We do not share your data with third parties, nor do we collect data from third parties. If a user of our product chooses to share, forward, or provide data to third-party connections, they are free to do so. But, the FluentBoards plugin does not autonomously collect or distribute user data
## Answer to Some Other Frequently Asked Questions
**Does FluentBoards track data?**
The FluentBoards website itself does not track user data. We only collect relevant user information that you intentionally provide to enhance the customer experience.
**Does FluentBoards use cookies for personal data storage?**
No, the FluentBoards WordPress plugin does not use cookies to store personal data. However, the website (fluentboards.com) does use cookies to improve the browsing experience. Users can accept or decline cookies by adjusting their browser settings.
**How to request data deletion?**
You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete the personal information that we hold. To exercise this right, please [contact us](<https://wpmanageninja.com/contact/>). End-users can also request to delete their data. However, to do so, request admin, only admin can delete.
