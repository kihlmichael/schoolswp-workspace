## Our Vision
We see ourselves as a group of problem solvers. So, rather than focusing on who we are, let’s shed some light on our vision. As a company, our motto is very simple
  * Put yourself in your user’s shoes
  * Address their pain points
  * Work tirelessly to resolve them.

![How WPManageNinja Works](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Group-1000016442-1-1024x785.webp)
Our main focus has always been to help small businesses bridge the gap between where they are and where they want to be! 
**And, we do not offer any plugins to our customers that we don’t use ourselves**. FluentBoards is no exception! 
**We have been using it since January 2023!** And we must say, as a project management solution within WordPress, FluentBoards already includes all the regular features of most Saas solutions.
