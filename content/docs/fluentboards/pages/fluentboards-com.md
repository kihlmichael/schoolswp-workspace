## Have a question? We’ve got answers!
How safe is FluentBoards?
FluentBoards is 100% self-hosted, giving you full control over your data. This means your information stays secure, entirely under your management, and even we cannot lock you out!
How many project views does FluentBoards offer?
FluentBoards currently offers four project views: Kanban (Free), List (Free), Calendar (Pro), and Table (Pro). More views are in the pipeline and will be launched soon.
Which integrations are currently available for FluentBoards?
FluentBoards currently integrates with FluentCRM, Fluent Forms, and Fluent Support. In addition, it supports both incoming and outgoing webhooks, allowing you to connect FluentBoards with virtually any tool and make it the central hub of your workflow. Over time, we’ll also add more integrations based on user feedback and recommendations.
Can I host my data outside?
Yes, absolutely! FluentBoards is fully self-hosted, giving you complete control over how your data is stored. If you prefer to host your data externally, you can use FluentBoards’ external storage integrations with Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3, DigitalOcean, and Backblaze B2.
I am already using another tool. Can I migrate swiftly?
Yes! FluentBoards supports smooth and easy migration, allowing you to bring all your projects with you—whether you’re using Trello, Asana, or any other project management tool.
What automation features does FluentBoards offer?
FluentBoards is actively expanding its automation features. Currently, it supports task cloning, task and project templates, default assignees, and recurring tasks. Additionally, if you’re a FluentCRM user, you can unlock even more automation possibilities thanks to its seamless integration with FluentCRM.
What is FluentRoadmap?
FluentRoadmap is a public roadmap-sharing add-on for FluentBoards that keeps all project stakeholders on the same page. It’s especially useful for software and digital businesses that want to involve their end users in the development process. To see it in action, [check out our FluentBoards roadmap](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>).
Will I need to pay extra to access FluentRoadmap?
No, you won’t have to pay extra to use FluentRoadmap. It comes free with the FluentBoards Pro version.
Do I need other Fluent plugins to use FluentBoards?
Absolutely not! FluentBoards is a standalone solution, giving you complete freedom & flexibility for everyday project management needs without requiring additional Fluent or [WPManageNinja products](<https://wpmanageninja.com/>).
Do I need coding skills to use FluentBoards?
Nope! FluentBoards is a super user-friendly product so you don’t need any coding skills or technical knowledge to manage your projects.
Can I share my feature suggestions?
Of course! We want FluentBoards to be designed exactly the way you want it. Share your [feature request here](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/7/>).
