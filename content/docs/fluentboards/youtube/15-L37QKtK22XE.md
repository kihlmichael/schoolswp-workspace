# Remote Team Collaboration Tips: How to Manage Remote Teams?

98% of professionals want remote work at least once in their careers because remote work has no commute, flexible hours, and total freedom. But are they ready for it? With great flexibility comes the challenges of miscommunication, workflow confusion, and even difficulty in performance tracking. Despite all these challenges, the right collaboration tips can keep your team productive, connected, and on track. Let's dive into the best ways to manage remote teamwork smoothly and stress-free. To collaborate better with your team, the first thing you need to do is manage things smartly.

Setting goals that are specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time bound smart is the key to successful remote team collaboration. Once you know what to achieve and how much time you have to do that, you can step into another great team collaboration technique, communication. Working remotely means your designer might be in New York or maybe your developer in China and your marketer in London. Different time zones, different work styles, and different mindset. That's a recipe for miscommunication.

So, how do we fix it? Simple. Use a centralized project management tool. First of all, it makes onboarding team members effortless despite being from different regions of the globe. With easy onboarding, you can manage their rules and limit their access to your project board so that they can see only what they are supposed to see. If your project management tool lets you secure back-end operations and manage everything from the front end like Fluent Boards, it's definitely a big plus.

Once you have your goals set and team members on boarded, you can focus on dividing your project work into multiple stages like planning, ongoing, done, and also achievable tasks. Then you can easily assign the right person to get it done with a clear deadline and priority. So no more guesswork. To guide them even better, you can write a description to complete the task perfectly and attach necessary documentation or files that can assist them in finishing it effortlessly.

Besides, to speed up the task completion, you can even set an estimated time and let the assenee log a time so that you can know how much time he is spending on the task. In case you're worried about tracking the performance of your remote worker, a tool like Fluent Boards can be a lifesaver for you. There are some other perks of having a project management tool. If any task takes longer than the expected time, you can divide the task into subtasks to get it done effortlessly.

To resolve any issue instantly, you can get realtime updates from your team members easily. In case you need to repeat any task regularly, you can convert it into a recurring task easily. Once any task is completed, easily move it to another stage in just a snap. While a tool can boost your team collaboration by many a mile, you need a good project manager or team lead to manage them and get your projects done in time. A successful remote collaboration stays on the top with someone who values all the unique perspectives and ideas coming to the table.

If you have a great team, the right person to lead, and a tool like Fluent Boards to make collaboration easier, managing a remote team becomes a lot easier.
