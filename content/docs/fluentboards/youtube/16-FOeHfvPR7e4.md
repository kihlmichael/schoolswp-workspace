# Common Project Management Mistakes You Should Avoid

Why do so many projects fail? It usually comes down to a few common but costly mistakes that add up. Lack of planning, mismanagement, not using the right tools, and failure to adapt scoop creep can easily throw any project off track. But avoiding them in your project isn't rocket science either. Today, I'm here to walk you through the common project management mistakes you should be careful of to make your project super successful. Let's start with a question.

Have you ever planned a trip without knowing the destination? I guess the answer is no. You need the same clarity for your projects. A common mistakes in project management is not defining the goals and objectives of the project. Before you even start, take a moment and ask yourself what exactly you are trying to achieve. Then outline your project and define its goals clearly. Once you know what to achieve, you can focus on the next question. How?

You know the success of any project heavily depends on the person in charge. A great project manager or team leader doesn't just manage task and teams. They inspire, motivate and drive results because they know how to structure a plan, allocate resources wisely and turn challenges into stepping stones. After defining your goals and choosing the right project manager, the next move is getting the right team member on board. You can provide all the resources and guidance, but without a skilled team, delivering on your promises becomes impossible.

This is why you must choose your team members wisely. Every team member you pick must bring the skills and expertise needed to move the project forward.
