## What is Fluent Forms?

It’s easy to use and has all the useful features to meet your day-to-day form creation needs. Whether you are a newbie or a tech expert, you’ll experience a no-code requirement for any form. 
Here are some special features of Fluent Forms:

  * Set conditional logic for user input

## How to configure Fluent Forms integration for your FluentBoards projects
Alright, now it’s time to enable and utilize the Fluent Forms integration to its fullest advantage for your FluentBoards projects.
Log into your WordPress dashboard and go to **Fluent Forms > Integrations** and ensure that you have enabled the FluentBoards integration.
![enable fluentboards integration in fluent forms ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/121-1024x492.webp)
Next, you’ll need to configure the FluentBoards integration feed in Fluent Forms for the specific project.
![update fluentboards integration feed in fluent forms](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/122-1024x327.webp)
Here, you’ll find the following options:
  * **Select Board:** Choose the board to which the form will be converted into a task
  * **Select Stage:** Determine the stage of the submitted task
  * **Select Labels:** Assign labels for the task
  * **Select Assignees:** Choose the team members responsible for completing the task
  * **Select FluentCRM Contact:** Associate the client with the task
  * **Select Priority:** Set the priority level of the task  

Plus, you need to configure the task title and description to receive your client input.
![configure fluentboards task title and description in fluent forms ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/125-1024x649.webp)
When you are done setting up everything then click **Save Feed**. Now it’s time to go live with your task form. This is how your task form will look to your clients and they can share their task details with you:
![fluent forms task form for fluenboards project](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/126-1024x689.webp)
This form will be automatically converted into a task in FluentBoards when your client clicks **Submit Form**.
![automatic conversion of form submission into task ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/127-1024x478.webp)
Now the task is ready to be managed in FluentBoards and your team will get a clear overview of their next target.
