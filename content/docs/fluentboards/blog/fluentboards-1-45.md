## CSV Export & Import
First things first – apologies for keeping you waiting! However, it’s better late than never!
![FluentBoards roadmap dashboard](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-4-1024x355.webp)[FluentBoards public roadmap](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>)!
In this update, we’ve finally delivered the **CSV export & import** feature you’ve been asking for a long time!
Previously, FluentBoards only allowed exporting and importing boards in JSON format. But with this update, migration has become even easier and smoother.
From now on, you can import any FluentBoards board in CSV format.
![You can import any FluentBoards board in CSV format.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-1-1024x269.webp)
Additionally, if you want to migrate your projects from other project management platforms (Notion, ClickUp, etc.), you can do it now in just a few clicks.
To do so, simply go to your All Boards dashboard, click on the three dots next to the ‘Add Board’ button, and select ‘Import From CSV’.
![To import any project in CSV format click on the three dots next to the ‘Add Board’ button, and select ‘Import From CSV’.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-2-1024x360.webp)
Map your data to ensure everything stays organized!
![Map your data to ensure everything stays organized!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-3-1024x581.webp)
Click the ‘Import Board’ button and here you go! 
![Click the ‘Import Board’ button to import your board](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-5-1024x404.webp)
**Note:** Before importing data from another platform, make sure the CSV file you downloaded is structured correctly.
## Custom Labels Support in Labels
Here comes another ‘You asked, we delivered’ feature!
![Here comes another 'You asked, we delivered' feature -Choose the Label Color](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-28-at-2.58.26 PM-1-1024x218.webp)From [FluentBoards Community](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/space/fluent-boards/home>)
[In the last update](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/fluentboards-1-4/>), we introduced task cover customization, and now we’re taking board label customization to the next level.
Previously, board labels were limited to a set of predefined colors.
![Previously, board labels were limited to a set of predefined colors.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-9-1024x532.webp)
But from now on, you can choose any color you want, allowing you to go limitless and stay true to your brand colors!
![But from now on, you can choose any color you want, allowing you to go limitless and stay true to your brand colors!
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-10-1024x429.webp)
Oho! Your task labels will now be more visually unique and meaningful than ever!
## Added Frontend Portal Link in admin bar for quick access
This feature is inspired by a recent request from our community group!
![From FluentBoards Community](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-28-at-5.48.21 PM-1024x254.webp)From [FluentBoards Community](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/space/fluent-boards/home>)
From now on, you can easily access your FluentBoards frontend portal from anywhere on your dashboard. If the frontend portal is enabled, simply hover over the home icon, and you’ll see a direct link to your frontend portal.
![If the frontend portal is enabled, simply hover over the home icon, and you'll see a direct link to your frontend portal.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-28-at-3.42.34 PM-1024x654.webp)
So, whether you’re working on a page, post, or any other plugin, jumping back to your project portal is now super simple!
**Note:** We’re planning to extend this feature to the website frontend too, so you won’t need to enter the website dashboard every time to access your portal. Stay tuned — maybe in the next update!
## A Couple of New Features for FluentRoadmap
As you know, FluentBoards offers a free public roadmap-sharing add-on, [FluentRoadmap](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/introducing-fluentroadmap/>), to help you bring everyone to the same table. And with this update, collaboration, and communication with stakeholders have become even more powerful
### 1\. FluentCRM integration for roadmap ideas
From now on, when a user submits an idea to your roadmap, you can directly add them to any predefined contact list in FluentCRM!
And how does this help?
Well, this unlocks countless opportunities to engage them further and make them feel valued. By leveraging FluentCRM’s automation, email campaigns, and other dynamic features, you can create connections that weren’t possible before!
However, to enable this feature, first go to the ‘Settings’ dashboard and open the ‘Roadmap’ option. You’ll see a new option called ‘Add new users to FluentCRM when an idea is submitted’.
![go to the ‘Settings’ dashboard and open the ‘Roadmap’ option. You’ll see a new option called ‘Add new users to FluentCRM when an idea is submitted’.
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-6-1024x472.webp)
Once you check this box, two new options will unfold. Simply select which tag you want to assign or which contact list you want to add them to (you can choose either or both), and save the settings.
![select which tag you want to assign or which contact list you want to add them to \(you can choose either or both\), and save the settings.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-7-1024x503.webp)
That’s it! 
![whenever someone submits an idea to your roadmap, they will automatically be added to your FluentCRM contact lists.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/unnamed-8-1024x724.webp)
Whenever someone submits an idea to your roadmap, they will automatically be added to your FluentCRM contact lists.
### 2\. Easily transition ideas across stages
Moving ideas between stages is now easier than ever!
Whenever someone submits an idea to your roadmap, you can change its stage directly from the front end! Yes, no need to go to the backend anymore!
![2025 02 27 16.26.21](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-02-27-16.26.21-1024x423.webp)
Just open the idea, click on the dropdown icon beside the stage, select the stage you want, and that’s it! The idea will move to the new stage instantly.

## RTL(Right-to-Left) Support provided
Here comes another great news!
FluentBoards now supports right-to-left (RTL) language formats!
So, if your website uses languages like **Arabic, Hebrew, Persian** , or any other RTL language, you can still enjoy a seamless project management experience without any limitations.
## Bug Fixes & Improvements
As you know, at FluentBoards, progress is all about balancing innovation with continuous improvement.
While introducing new features excites us, we’re equally committed to perfecting the tools you already trust. Here’s a quick look at the bug fixes and enhancements included in this update:
  * **Improvement:** More translation strings added
  * **Improvement:** Enabled 24-hour time format support
  * **Improvement:** Task link copy via the ‘Copy Link’ button
  * **Improvement:** Redesigned Features & Modules with improved UI/UX
  * **Fixed:** Multisite Issue
  * **Fixed:** Date diff discrepancy in activities
  * **Fixed:** External storage integration issues
  * **Fixed** : Width overflow issue in task description
  * **Fixed:** Margin issues in the frontend portal report
  * **Fixed:** Logged time disappearing when moving tasks between boards
  * **Fixed:** Task created from FluentCRM automation default priority issue
  * Other Improvements & Bug Fixes
