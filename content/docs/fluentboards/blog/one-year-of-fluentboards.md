## How it all started?
“With hundreds of project management tools already out there, why add another one to the mix?”
Yes, like you, that was the exact question on our minds when we first considered bringing FluentBoards to everyone!
But immediately, a second thought followed— “If those tools were truly solving all our problems… why did we feel the need to build our own?”
Makes sense, right?
So, we rolled up our sleeves and did what we do best!
After using FluentBoards internally for over six months, refining it through real-world challenges and feedback, we launched the beta version in March 2024. 
And their response? Well, it was beyond our imagination! 
The excitement, the feedback, and the shared frustration with existing tools showed us that this wasn’t just our problem. We realized the absence of a project management tool that’s simple yet flexible, powerful yet easy to use, built for WordPress, and affordable is real! 
So, we set out to solve the problem, hand in hand with our beta testers!
They explored, they broke things (thanks!), they shared honest feedback. 
We listened. We refined. We improved. 
And finally, on June 3rd, 2024, we went live for the world.
We’ve shared the full story — [How and Why We Built FluentBoards](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/introducing-fluentboards/>). If you’re interested in digging deeper, give it a read!
## The Journey Since Then…
Wow! What a ride it’s been!
A WordPress project management tool that was initially built to solve WPManageNinja’s internal project management chaos has come a very long way…
The product got better with every update, we’ve surpassed many milestones we once only dreamed of, and, most importantly, thanks to your continuous support and feedback, the journey of love continues!
Here’s a quick look at what we’ve achieved together:
### FluentBoards – the #1 Project Management Plugin on WordPress.org!
![FluentBoards - the #1 Project Management Plugin on WordPress.org!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/White-and-Blue-Professional-Modern-Technology-Pitch-Deck-Presentation-1-1024x576.webp)
Go ahead and search “project management” on [WordPress.org](<http://wordpress.org>). What’s the first plugin you see?
Yep, it’s FluentBoards, right at the top!
In just one year, FluentBoards has climbed its way to become the number one project management plugin on WordPress.org. 
We’ve officially surpassed 719 other plugins in the same category, including industry veterans that have been around for nearly a decade. And, we’re incredibly proud.
But more than that – we’re grateful!
Grateful for your trust. Grateful for your feedback. 
Grateful for every upvote, download, and message of support.
### 4,000+ active installations, 40,000+ downloads & counting!
Let’s take a moment to appreciate this:
Over 4,000 teams now rely on FluentBoards to run their daily project management workflows.
And here’s the wild part—we’re not even the biggest team using it!
Some of the teams using FluentBoards are even larger than our own 120+ member crew! And, it puts us further down the leaderboard of the largest FluentBoards users.
Tough pill to swallow? 
Maybe! But honestly, we’re smiling through it. 
Because this just proves one thing – **FluentBoards is built to scale**. And, from solopreneurs to enterprise teams, it’s working well.
What about the total download count?
Well, we’ve already smashed through the 40,000 milestone, with no signs of slowing down.
### A Thriving Community of Experts, Users & Well-Wishers
From day one, we’ve said it loud and clear: **FluentBoards is designed by users, built by us!**
And we’ve meant every word!
We’ve always wanted to keep our users close, not just as customers, but as collaborators. 
While WPManageNinja has traditionally used Facebook groups to bring users together, for FluentBoards, we wanted to create something more exclusive.
Thanks to our very own community management plugin, FluentCommunity, building that space became incredibly seamless. We launched a private, secure FluentBoards community, centered around simplicity, speed, and data privacy.
And today, it’s home to 2,500+ active members engaging in meaningful discussions, sharing feedback, and shaping the future of FluentBoards, together.
Haven’t joined yet?
👉 [Be a part of our community](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/space/fluent-boards/home>)!
### 14 Powerful Product Updates—Driven by You!
From the very beginning, our motto has been simple: **“Your ideas. Our execution.”**
And that spirit is alive and thriving.
You kept the ideas flowing, and our developers?
They’ve been on it, working tirelessly (and proudly) to turn your suggestions into real, usable features.
Since launch, we’ve rolled out 13 impactful updates, each one shaped by your feedback and built with care.
Here are just a few highlights of what we’ve built together:
  * Calendar View
  * Due Date Reminders (Daily Summary emails)
  * Custom Fields to fit your workflow
  * FluentRoadmap – public roadmap sharing add-on (Free)
  * Board Export/Import for flexibility
  * Recurring Tasks to automate routine work
  * Default Stage Assignees for smarter workflows
  * External Storage Provider integration (Backblaze, Digital Ocean, Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3)
  * Subtask Group for better organization

👉 [Full Changelog](<https://fluentboards.com/docs/changelog/>)
And the best part?
We’re just getting started. Our devs have no plans to hit pause anytime soon! 
### Every question answered – 750+ Support Tickets Resolved!
Features make a product great. Marketing draws users in. 
But exceptional customer support? 
That’s what sets a product apart and keeps users coming back.
That’s why we can’t thank our support superheroes enough. They’ve been the silent champions behind every smooth user experience, every solved problem, and every happy customer.
Over the past one year, our support team has resolved 700+ tickets, staying true to our commitment of providing 24/7 priority support—**quick, reliable, and human**.
### 40+ Blogs & Video Tutorials—Built to Support You
Breaking into the crowded project management space wasn’t easy!
But our marketing team rose to the challenge and made the first year of FluentBoards a great success!
To support you on your project management journey, we stayed active across our social channels, shared helpful emails, and consistently delivered valuable resources to help you get the most out of FluentBoards.
Over the past 12 months, we’ve published [40+ blog posts](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/>), a balanced mix of practical guides, product updates, and project management insights.
We also created a growing library of exclusive video tutorials to walk you through features, workflows, and real use cases.
👉 [Our YouTube Channel](<https://www.youtube.com/@WPManageNinja>)
And we’re just getting started. In the coming months, we’re committed to delivering even more content that’s focused on you, your needs, your challenges, and your success.
## Much Done, Much More to Do
A project management solution:
  * That’s simple, clutter-free, and packed with just the right features

  * That brings powerful, SaaS-level project management capabilities into WordPress, at a far more accessible price

That’s the dream FluentBoards has carried since day one.
Now, as we kickstart our second year, we’re more committed than ever to making that vision a reality.
So, the journey of awesomeness continues with you by our side.
We’ll get better. We’ll grow stronger. And together, we’ll become even more impactful.
Thank you once again for your unwavering love and support. Let’s keep building the project management solution our WordPress community truly deserves.
