Tick-tock, tick-tock! The clock is ticking and 2025 is just around the corner!
After a spooktacular Halloween deal, our record-breaking Black Friday season, and with the sweet sound of Christmas bells ringing (alongside our [Christmas Day deal](<https://fluentboards.com/discount-deal/>)), the FluentBoards team is beyond thrilled to step into 2025. 
It all started on June 3, 2024, just six months ago! Yet, what a journey it’s been! The love, support, and trust we’ve received in this short time have been nothing short of amazing.
Our hearts are full—full of gratitude, achievement, and an unstoppable drive to achieve even more.
**And here’s the truth** : YOU made this possible. Every milestone we hit, every achievement we unlocked, it’s all you. Thank you!
Curious about FluentBoards’ mesmerizing journey?
Let’s take a quick walk down memory lane to see what we’ve achieved together and what exciting things waiting for us in 2025.
## How It All Started?
Well, we’ve shared the story behind FluentBoards many times before, even published a [detailed blog](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/introducing-fluentboards/>) on it. But still, wouldn’t it be great to revisit where it all began before diving into our year-end (or, to be exact, six-month) review?
So, here’s how it all started:
  * We (at [WPManageNinja](<https://wpmanageninja.com/>)) have been FluentBoards alongside Trello as our internal project management tools since June 2023. Why both? Well, while FluentBoards handled things within WordPress, Trello forced us to switch platforms constantly. And let’s face it, that wasn’t exactly productive.

  * When **Trello announced a 10-member limit** on its free version, the bell rang for us! With a team of 120+ members, this decision would surely bring unnecessary expenses to the table. So, we asked ourselves: Why not shift completely to FluentBoards and turn it into our very own “[WordPress-native Trello](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/trello-alternatives-for-wordpress/>)”?

  * But wait! Was it just us struggling with platform switching and rising costs? The moment we made the switch, we realized the answer: **No, we weren’t alone!** Many in the WordPress community were in the same boat. That’s when we made a bold decision: Why not offer this tool to the entire WordPress community?

  * Before launching publicly, we wanted real feedback. After all, a project management tool is one of the most critical tools for any business. So, instead of overloading it with unnecessary features born from our own assumptions, we opened FluentBoards for beta testing **in March 2024**.

  * And the response? Phenomenal! **Over 1,000 beta testers** eagerly joined our exclusive community (built using [FluentCommunity](<https://fluentcommunity.co/>)!) and got their hands on the tool. Over the next three months, they gave us invaluable feedback, suggested tons of features, and pushed us to design a tool truly tailored to real user needs.

  * Within those three months, we rolled out **10 major product updates** and launched over 20 brand-new features!

  * Finally, on **June 3rd, 2024** , after countless tweaks, refinements, and a whole lot of love, FluentBoards, the simplest project management tool, went live for everyone!

And, how was the rest of the journey?
Well, what happened next is nothing short of incredible! Let’s unfold the journey together!

## 3,000+ Active Installations & 20,000+ Overall Downloads
Look at these numbers! Isn’t it something to be proud of?
Over 3,000 businesses rely on FluentBoards to manage their daily project management needs. And you know what? We’re not even the biggest team (120+ members) using it!
There are many large teams using FluentBoards every day—some even bigger than ours (though we can’t reveal their names due to privacy concerns). These teams rely on FluentBoards for their critical project management needs, pushing us further down the list of the largest FluentBoards users!
Tough pill to swallow, right?
And the overall downloads? Well, they’ve already crossed the 20,000 mark!
## 8 Impactful Product Updates!
“**Your ideas, our executions** ” has been our motto since day one, and this journey continues!
You’ve never run out of ideas, and our developers? Well, they haven’t had much time to rest either (and they’re happy about it, honestly).
Thanks to your valuable feedback, we’ve rolled out 8 impactful product updates, including 5 major updates since FluentBoards launched. Here are some of the standout features we’ve delivered so far:
  * WP Editor in Board Description
  * Convert Task to Subtask
  * Calendar View
  * Due Date Reminder (Daily Summary)
  * Custom Fields
  * User Profile
  * FluentRoadmap
  * Board Export/Import
  * Recurring Task
  * External Storage Provider
  * Default Stage Assignee
  * View-only Member Role

And guess what? Our developers have no plans to hit pause anytime soon!

## What’s coming next? 
Yes, we know—2025 is going to be even more challenging for us. After an incredibly successful first six months, one thing is certain: the pressure of meeting expectations and the drive to surpass our achievements will be immense.
But as a team, we are wholeheartedly ready to embrace these challenges with open arms. Our marketing team is gearing up to be more impactful, our support team is preparing to be even faster and more responsive, and the Dev team?
Well, they’re already hard at work! And with your support, they’re planning to take significant steps toward delivering an even more comprehensive project management solution. Here’s what’s currently in the pipeline (with more to come!):
  * Board Categories
  * Board Templates
  * Gantt Chart
  * Email Customization
  * Calendar Integrations (Google Calendar, etc.)
  * Advanced Task Automation Workflow
  * More External Storage Providers
  * And More!

**Note:** As always, it’s you who shapes the future of FluentBoards. We encourage you to [share your feature requests](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/>) on our roadmap so we can continue to build and improve with your needs in mind.
## Miles to Go Before We Sleep!
A project management solution that’s simple, free from unnecessary features, yet equipped with all the essential ones; one that brings all the SaaS project management functionalities into WordPress at a much more affordable price—that’s the dream FluentBoards has carried since its creation.
As we step into 2025, we hope to take significant strides toward this vision and continue to meet your expectations.
With that, our year-end review comes to a close. Before we finish, we want to extend our heartfelt thanks to you for joining us on this journey and standing by us through every high and low.
Wishing you an early Happy New Year 2025 and Happy FluentBoarding! Cheers!
