## First Things First: What Is FluentBoards?
FluentBoards is a fully native project management solution built exclusively for WordPress. It lets you plan, track, and manage all your projects—right from your WordPress dashboard, with zero learning curve.
**Tagline:** The Simplest Project Management Plugin for WordPress
**Why FluentBoards stands out:**
  * **No platform switching hassle:** Manage everything directly from your WordPress dashboard
  * **No limits, no surprises** : Enjoy unlimited projects, teammates, and file sharing
  * **No scale penalty:** No per-user or per-project pricing
  * **Dynamic integrations** : Easily connect with your other favorite tools
  * **Full data ownership:** Your data stays 100% in your control, always

## The Birth of FluentBoards!
With hundreds of project management tools out there, why build another?
Great question!
Well, we didn’t build this just for the sake of it.
There were strong reasons behind the creation of this plugin, reasons rooted in our own experience.
Like hundreds of other businesses, we’ve been running our operations on WordPress since day one.
We started with a tiny team of 4–5 people.
But things have changed a lot. Especially over the last 2–3 years.
Our business has grown, and so has our team. Today, we’re proud to have a team of 100+ members.
At that time, we were using Trello. 
But since their pricing is member-based, managing a team of this size with multiple ongoing projects quickly became expensive, and the cost kept climbing!
Besides, to use Trello, we had to leave our WordPress dashboard every time. And that was a real hassle!
So, we knew it was time to switch.
Curious to learn more?
Here’s our full story 👉 [Why we moved from Trello to FluentBoards](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/trello-alternatives-for-wordpress/>)
Naturally, we started looking for a project management solution that could bring order to our growing operations, without breaking the bank.
Yes, there were plenty of solid SAAS tools in the market, packed with features! 
But unfortunately, most of them came with the exact issues we were hoping to leave behind:
  * They weren’t made for WordPress users
  * The pricing was outrageous

We tried the WordPress project management plugins out there, too. 
But most either lacked essential features, had a clunky interface, or simply didn’t play well with the rest of our plugin stack.
So we thought—why not build the tool we’ve always wanted?
A project management plugin with:
  * Modern features
  * A clean, intuitive UI
  * Seamless team collaboration
  * And most importantly, built natively for WordPress

That’s how real problems sparked the need, and eventually, FluentBoards was born.

## Long Story Short: Why We Built It?
Throughout the 9-year journey of WPManageNinja, one thing has remained constant! 
We’ve never stopped asking **“why”**!
And here are all the whys that made FluentBoards a reality –
### 1\. FluentBoards is the solution we needed
We’ve already said it: F**luentBoards wasn’t just built for you. It was built for us, too.**
When you’re managing a team of 120+ people with dozens of ongoing projects, an efficient project management tool isn’t just helpful—it’s absolutely essential.
Ironically, though, most tools on the market just didn’t work for us!
  * Most tools are SaaS-based, meaning we had to leave our WordPress dashboard to manage projects.

  * WordPress plugins fell short! They either lacked essential features or were overloaded with things we didn’t need.

  * Most tools had high costs! Besides, scalability was restricted as per-user pricing models punished growth.

  * Managing projects outside WordPress always made us feel like we were risking data loss.

So, we built our own solution—FluentBoards—to break free from these limitations and finally manage projects the way we needed to.. 
**Bonus info:** We’ve been using FluentBoards since June 2023, and since then, our team’s **productivity has increased by 200%!**
### 2\. Our community deserved something better
Were we the only ones feeling this way?
To be honest, we had our doubts. 
Project management isn’t exactly a hot topic in the WordPress space, and that made us a bit uncertain.
So, to test the waters, we quietly offered FluentBoards to a handful of our users.
Their reaction?
They were over the moon! Honestly, we didn’t expect such overwhelming appreciation.
And when we finally rolled out the beta version publicly, everything became clear:
The problem wasn’t that people didn’t want to [manage projects within WordPress](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/how-to-manage-projects-within-wordpress/>)!
The problem was that there simply weren’t any good options.
Fast forward to today, FluentBoards is the fastest-growing project management plugin for WordPress. Here’s [how far we’ve come in just one year](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/one-year-of-fluentboards/>)!
### 3\. Another step closer to our goal
When we say, **“We want to help small businesses grow,”** we truly mean it.
Take a closer look at our plugins, and you’ll see that we’re on a mission to build an **affordable, powerful ecosystem** tailored for small businesses.
We created **Fluent Forms** to simplify lead generation. **FluentCRM** was built to make email marketing accessible and budget-friendly. And every other product we’ve launched solves real, native WordPress problems.
In this mission, **FluentBoards** is the latest addition to the Fluent ecosystem, bringing project management into the mix and making our toolkit more complete.
It’s designed to make our users’ lives simpler, more organized, and truly efficient.
## Key Features of FluentBoards 
Here’s what you can expect from FluentBoards:
#### **Better project management**
FluentBoards lets you create unlimited tasks, onboard your entire team, allocate tasks with precision, monitor progress, and ensure the success of every project in the most organized way.
  * **Unlimited projects and tasks:** You can run an unlimited number of projects and include as many tasks as you like, indicating there are no restrictions on the scale or scope of your projects  
  

  * **Infinite Assignees:** Distribute tasks among as many team members as required, maximizing the utilization of your workforce  
  

  * **Multi-stage workflow:** Break down complex projects into multiple stages and track progress seamlessly

![multi stage workflow in fluentboards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-12.06.18 PM-1024x50.webp)
  * **CRM Contact Management:** FluentCRM users can seamlessly integrate their CRM contacts into any task or board, ensuring their team has access to essential client details

![fluentcrm contact management in fluentboards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-12.09.44 PM.webp)
  * **Easy data migration:** Export your project data from **Asana** and **Trello** in JSON format and simplify the process of importing data into FluentBoards

#### **Enhanced visual navigation**
With FluentBoards you can turn your hectic work environment into a stress-free workflow. Visualize your work status in a way that boosts motivation and productivity. 
  * **Kanban view:** View your entire workflow in a dynamic Kanban board and track task progress as they move through different stages

![project management kanban board view in fluentboards](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-1.04.36 PM-1-1024x471.webp)
  * **List view:** See all tasks in a list format with vital task information

![project tasks in list view in fluentboards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-1.08.29 PM-1024x444.webp)
  * **Dynamic switching:** Toggle between Kanban and list views to visualize your tasks in your way

  * **Smart labeling:** Apply colored labels to your tasks and tag tasks for easy identification 

  * **Customizable background:** Customize the board background and add vibrancy to your work environment 

![customize kanban board background ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-1.03.30 PM-1024x526.webp)
#### **Efficient goal management**
You cannot successfully accomplish any project without setting goals and tracking progress. With this in mind, FluentBoards goal management features are developed to ensure that you hit your goals with high precision.
  * **Due date:** Set task deadlines and provide clear guidance to assignees on their targets

  * **Priority management:** Assign task priority (Low, Medium, or High) and inform the assignee about task importance 

  * **Advanced filtering:** Filter tasks by assignee, due date, priority, etc. and get an immediate work status update

![advanced filtering options in fluentboards](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/convert-484x1024.webp)
  * ****Reporting dashboard:**** Receive a comprehensive visual report on task completion, priority, and team performance for any project in the form of pie, doughnut, and bar charts. 

![task reporting dashboard in fluentboards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-2.50.18 PM-1-1024x474.webp)
  * **Progress tracking:** Track every task progress and identify drawbacks

#### Improved team collaboration
As the saying goes, ‘United we stand, divided we fall’; project management is all about achieving goals through shared efforts. FluentBoards ensures that you reach your project goals with maximum team effort.
  * **Personalized dashboard:** Every team member will get a personal dashboard with individual tasks to make sure they stay focused on their targets

![personalized dashboard in fluentboards for project team member](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-4.49.22 PM-1-1024x426.webp)
  * **Email notification:** Receive instant email notification for every important project event 

  * **Real-time communication:** Stay in sync with your team, work in real-time, check updates, and solve problems together

  * **File sharing:** Share any type of file and give your team members immediate access to important documents 

#### Improved security & less admin work
Security threats and administrative burdens can pose a significant barrier to your project’s success. Keeping that in mind, FluentBoards ensures you run risk-free projects and invest more time in productive work.
  * **Data privacy:** Keep sensitive project information private and control the sharing of data

  * **Dynamic admin dashboard:** Keep track of all your projects from a centralized hub 

![dynamic admin dashboard in fluentbooking ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-5.55.00 PM-1-1024x638.webp)
  * **Set member role & permission:** Set member roles and manage user permission for any project

![set member roles and permission for any project in fluentboards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-5.59.14 PM-1024x348.webp)
  * **Email preference management:** Manage email preference for any task or board and set email notification events

![set email preference for different project events ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-16-at-6.02.51 PM-1024x517.webp)
  * **Card view preference:** Select the elements that you want to highlight on any task card

## What Integrations FluentBoards is Offering? 
Currently, FluentBoards supports three major integrations: FluentCRM, Fluent Forms, and Fluent Support. But rest assured that we’ll add more integrations in the coming days.   
  
Now let me give you a quick overview of the major integrations:
  * **FluentCRM:** The [FluentCRM](<https://fluentcrm.com/>) integration is going to be a game-changer for WordPress project management. You can directly add your clients to relevant projects and make client-based project management more fruitful with automation power.

Read [this article](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/how-fluentcrm-complement-fluentboards/>) and learn more about the FluentCRM integration
  * **Fluent Forms:**[Fluent Forms](<https://fluentforms.com/>) integration is developed to make task management in WordPress more dynamic than ever. All you need to do is create a task submission form in Fluent Forms and when the client submits the form it’ll be automatically converted into a task.

Read [this documentation](<https://fluentboards.com/docs/fluentboards-integration-with-fluent-forms/>) and learn more about the Fluent Forms integration
  * **Fluent Support:** With the [Fluent Support](<https://fluentsupport.com/>) integration you’ll unlock a new way of managing your support tickets in FluentBoards. When a client submits a ticket you can add that ticket to FluentBoards and manage your client ticket more efficiently.
