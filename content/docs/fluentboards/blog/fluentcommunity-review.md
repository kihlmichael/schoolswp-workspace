## What is FluentCommunity?
[FluentCommunity](<https://fluentcommunity.co/>) is a versatile WordPress plugin that allows you to create and manage communities of any size directly on your WordPress site.

## The Story of FluentBoards Team: Why and How We Started Using FluentCommunity
![WPManageNinja is now powering 950K+ businesses](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-21-1024x512.webp)
At our parent company, [WPManageNinja](<https://wpmanageninja.com/>), our users are our top priority. We believe in staying connected with them and actively listening to their feedback.
To facilitate this, we’ve established dedicated private groups on Facebook for each of our products.
But do these groups truly meet our needs?
Well, to be honest, not completely!
  * Many of our users aren’t comfortable using Facebook
  * We’ve to switch platforms to communicate with our users
  * We receive a lot of unnecessary join requests, and verifying them is time-consuming

And most importantly, as you know, social media can be a major time sink!
So, the search for a better alternative didn’t come to us overnight! We had been considering it for a long time. And, when we decided to launch FluentBoards, the need for a more specialized community became clearer!
Before we went live with FluentBoards publicly, we wanted a few beta testers to try it first.
We wanted their reviews, insights, and feature requests, and to design FluentBoards based on their feedback. So, initially, we thought about creating a separate Facebook group.
But here’s where our challenges peaked!
  * We aimed to build an exclusive community, where only select members could join. However, maintaining transparency and ensuring only the right people had access to that Facebook group turned out to be a complex task.

  * Since we planned to develop FluentBoards based on our beta testers’ feedback, keeping a close eye on their comments was crucial. It needed to be real-time communication. However, tracking everything on Facebook was difficult. Plus, since we run everything on WordPress, constantly switching platforms could waste our valuable time.

  * We also wanted to give our users VIP treatment! But how could a Facebook group deliver that?

So, we began searching for a better alternative. And guess what? 
The solution was right in front of us!
We already had a community plugin we’d been developing for years and were using it as an internal communication hub for over 100 employees, engaging and sharing thoughts on different topics.
And, we decided to leverage it to create a personalized, exclusive community for our beta testers. And the result?

## Build Your First Community in Minutes—It’s That Easy!
FluentCommunity has made creating and managing a community easier than ever!
Once you install and activate the plugin, you’ll see it on your dashboard.
Now, go ahead and click on it. You’ll be taken to a window that looks like this!
![FluentCommunity Dashboard](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-25-1024x209.webp)
Everything is neat and clean—no one has set foot here yet; you’re the first one! **Congratulations**!
To create your first community, click on the (+) icon next to the “Get Started” option!
![To create your first community, click on the \(+\) icon next to the "Get Started" option!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-26-1024x285.webp)
Add the necessary information: your community name, a short description, and the slug you want to use. Remember, in FluentCommunity, **“community” refers to “spaces”**!
![Add the necessary information: your community name, a short description, and the slug you want to use.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-27-1024x321.webp)
Now it’s time to make some necessary settings! To do this, scroll down a bit.
Decide whether you want this community to be public, private, or secret, and choose who can post in it. Set a featured image and click the “Create” button!
![Decide whether you want this community to be public, private, or secret, and choose who can post in it. Set a featured image and click the "Create" button!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-28-1024x630.webp)
Hurrah! Your new community is now ready! Go ahead, add members, and start your first discussion!
![Hurrah! Your new community is now ready!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/unnamed-29-1024x520.webp)
So, how much time did it take to create your first community? **Just 5 minutes** at most, right? Yes, creating an exclusive community is now that simple and fast!
## Key Features of FluentCommunity
Okay, enough chit-chat! Now, let’s dive into what features FluentCommunity offers and how it can help you build and manage online communities and courses.
For your convenience and better understanding, we’ve divided all the FluentCommunity features into two groups – 
### Community features of FluentCommunity
As you might predict, FluentCommunity is designed with a primary focus on simplifying community building and management. It provides every essential feature you need, including support for unlimited members, groups, courses, and admins.
![feed](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Feed-1024x702.webp)
Here’s a sneak peek of what it offers:
  * **Community dashboard** : A centralized dashboard to monitor and manage community activity, track member engagement, and assess overall performance in real-time

  * **Leaderboard:** Highlight top contributors and inspire friendly competition among members with a ranking system

  * **Activity feeds:** Create and oversee group discussions, collaborations, and networking, all through an interactive newsfeed

  * **Profile setup:** Let members build and customize their profiles to reflect their interests and contributions within the community

  * **Groups:** Organize and manage groups for targeted discussions, collaboration, and community interaction, ensuring a focused environment

  * **Posts:** Members can share updates, insights, and announcements with the community through interactive posts

  * **Chats:** FluentCommunity offers a built-in chat feature for members to engage in instant, private conversations

### Course features of FluentCommunity
FluentCommunity isn’t just about community features—it’s a great tool for online instructors and course providers too! 
![course](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Course-1024x700.webp)
It helps you create an engaging learning environment where students can interact, collaborate, and participate beyond just studying content. It brings everything together in one convenient place for your learners.
  * **Course builder:** Easily create lessons, quizzes, and assignments in one spot with our simple Course Builder, making learning experiences more organized and engaging.

  * **Progress tracking** : Keep track of how your students are doing with detailed progress reports. You can monitor course completion, quiz results, and overall performance to help them stay on course.

  * **Lesson discussions:** Encourage collaboration and engagement by allowing students to participate in lesson discussions, making learning more interactive and connected.

### Other key features
FluentCommunity goes beyond just providing community and course features—it gives you all the tools needed for your community to flourish, without requiring any complex coding. It also offers – 
  * Customer dashboard
  * Real-time Notification
  * Unlimited file sharing
  * Media storage
  * Role manager
  * Dark/ Light mode
  * Global search
  * Member directories
  * Data security
  * Privacy controls
