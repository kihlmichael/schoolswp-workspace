Time flies.
And it’s funny how a year can feel both long and suddenly over.
When we started 2025, we knew this would be a year full of reviews, feedback, and, of course, feature requests.
But we made a conscious decision not to rush.
Instead of chasing big releases right away, we chose to move steadily and strategically. We wanted to build a strong base that would allow FluentBoards to scale smoothly and confidently in the future. 
Now, as the calendar flips toward the end of the year, we must say: we quietly achieved more in 2025 than we expected!
And none of it would have been possible without you. 
Let’s look back at everything we achieved together this year!
## How Was 2025 for FluentBoards in a Nutshell?
2025 marked a year of steady and meaningful growth for FluentBoards, both as a product and as a team! What started as a simple idea to make project management easier has grown into a tool trusted by teams managing real work every day. 
![fluentboards year in review. png](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Fluentboards-year-in-review.-png-1024x624.webp)
Here’s a quick snapshot of what we achieved this year:
### 5,000+ Active Installations
As we’ve shared many times before, [FluentBoards was built](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/introducing-fluentboards/>) for our own internal project management needs. We were tired of the high pricing and unnecessary complexity of the tools we were using at the time.
So in June 2024, when we stepped into an already crowded project management space, our mindset was clear and deliberate.
We wanted to build a tool that:
  * Is simple and comes with almost zero learning curve
  * Doesn’t create unnecessary limits on members, projects, or file sharing
  * Remains affordable for growing teams

Still, we had our doubts!
With so many capable alternatives available, we often asked ourselves: who would actually choose FluentBoards?
Well, FluentBoards’ simplicity echoed louder than we expected!
Today, **more than 5,000 businesses** use FluentBoards to manage their project chaos, making it one of the fastest-growing WordPress project management plugins.
On top of that, total product downloads have already crossed 70,000!
And, once again, thank you! You made all of this possible.
**Enjoying FluentBoards?**
Don’t forget to give us a [five-star review on WordPress](<https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/fluent-boards/reviews/#new-post>). Your kind words will fuel us as we continue this journey forward.
### 15 Product Updates
First things first: huge kudos to our development team.
From a development standpoint, 2025 was all about shipping meaningful updates, improving performance, and paying close attention to how FluentBoards was being used in real-world scenarios.
The approach was simple.
  * Put ourselves in users’ shoes
  * Listen carefully
  * Understand real needs
  * Refine what already exists and ship new features based on clear priorities

But that doesn’t mean we slowed down!
Development continued, and the team kept shipping meaningful new features. Across**14 product updates** (8 major), we shipped over 40 new features, each designed to improve real-world workflows.

  9. Custom color support in labels
  10. CSV Export and Import
  11. Global UTF-8 Support for comments
  12. Task View Drawer for list & calendar view

  14. Filter by task status

  16. Date Selection options for time logs
  17. Customizable Tabs in my tasks dashboard
  18. Custom color support for the stage background

## Top Ten Most Requested Features Added to FluentBoards in 2025
From our early beta testing days to now, FluentBoards has continued to evolve based on your feature requests. 2025 followed the same path, with a clear goal of addressing as many roadmap requests as possible.
**Note:** As mentioned earlier, our focus this year was on stability, sustainability, and strengthening the foundation. We prioritized fine-tuning the core experience before rolling out major features, so a few of the most upvoted requests weren’t shipped. Rest assured, they’re either in planning or active development and will be coming soon.
Every feature we delivered this year came either from your requests on our roadmap or from discussions in our community group. And here are the top ten requested features we successfully delivered:
### 1\. Board Folderization for Better Project Management
![Board Folderization for Better Project Management](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-07-24-at-2.41.05-PM-1536x564-1-1024x376.webp)
Until now, this was the most upvoted feature on our roadmap, and we have successfully delivered it. This feature helps you categorize projects into folders for better clarity and structure.
### 2\. CSV Export & Import 
![CSV Export & Import ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/unnamed-2-1536x540-1-1024x360.webp)
Another vital and highly requested feature we delivered this year is board export and import in CSV format. With this in place, you can seamlessly move your projects in or out of FluentBoards.
### 3\. More External Storage Integration
![More External Storage Integration](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/unnamed-60-1024x456.webp)
Previously, external storage options were limited to Cloudflare R2 and Amazon S3. Based on your requests, we’ve added two more storage providers this year: Backblaze and DigitalOcean Spaces.
### 4\. Pin Boards to Main Nav / Quick Access for Frequently Used Boards
![Pin Boards to Main Nav / Quick Access for Frequently Used Boards](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-05-30-at-11.35.07-AM-1536x426-1-1024x284.webp)
After being in the planning stage, the highly requested Pinned Boards feature was finally launched this year.
### 5\. Outgoing Webhooks
![screenshot 2025 09 19 at 10.17.48 am 1536x520](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-10.17.48-AM-1536x520-1-1024x347.webp)
To further empower FluentBoards as the central hub of your workflow, we introduced [Outgoing Webhooks](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/fluentboards-outgoing-webhooks/>) this year. This allows you to automatically push updates from FluentBoards and connect your boards and tasks with your favorite tools like Slack, Discord, Zapier, and more.
### 6\. Subtask Group Like Trello Checklist
![screenshot 2025 05 20 at 3.22.00 pm](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-05-20-at-3.22.00-PM-1024x435.webp)
Another small but impactful feature, Subtask Groups, went live this year. Instead of displaying all subtasks in a single long list under a parent task, you can now organize them into logical groups such as Research, Design, Review, or any custom label that fits your workflow.
### 7\. Task Cloning
![Task Cloning](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-07-24-at-4.07.02-PM-1024x549.webp)
As we plan the future of FluentBoards, automation is a key part of our vision, and the task cloning feature is one of many more to come. This feature lets you duplicate any task and easily customize it, saving time and eliminating the hassle of creating everything from the ground up.
### 8\. New Project View: Table View
![New Project View: Table View](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-10.22.26-AM-1536x810-1-1024x540.webp)
This year, we introduced a brand-new project view, Table View, to make project planning and execution in FluentBoards even smoother and more efficient. Previously, you had three project view options to choose from: Kanban, Calendar, and List.
### 9\. Task and Subtask Reminder
![Task and Subtask Reminder](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-4.41.44-PM-1536x1078-1-1024x719.webp)
FluentBoards email notifications became smarter and more powerful this year with the introduction of Task and Subtask Reminders. With this feature, you can choose to receive notifications exactly when you need them—30 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, or even 1 week before the due date.
### 10\. Task Watcher for Enhanced Stakeholder Management
![Task Watcher for Enhanced Stakeholder Management](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-07-25-at-2.41.42-PM-1024x470.webp)
This feature allows you to add anyone to a task as a watcher, keeping them in the loop with all task updates and notifications, without giving them the ability to make changes to the task.
### Bonus: Basic Reporting Is Free for All
Team is working actively on advanced reporting, and we hope you’ll see notable improvements soon. In the meantime, based on user requests, we’ve made basic reporting completely free for everyone, which was previously available only in the premium version.
Got a feature in mind? 
Don’t forget to [submit it to our roadmap](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>)!
## A Project Management Tool Is Never “Done”
That’s everything we achieved in 2025, in a nutshell.
Oh, what a year it has been!
However, before we turn the page, we want to assure you that 2026 will be even more exciting!
In 2025, FluentBoards focused on strengthening its foundation. 
In 2026, we plan to accelerate, grow, and deliver features that create even greater impact.
To give you a glimpse of what’s coming next, here are some of the major improvements you can expect in the near future:
  * Gantt chart or timeline view
  * Stronger integration with FluentCRM
  * Advanced reporting options
  * Built-in automation improvements in FluentBoards
  * FluentCart integration
  * And more

And the growth?
Well, if this momentum continues, we are confident we’ll soon be able to say this out loud:
_FluentBoards isn’t just one of the most popular WordPress project management plugins. It’s the most popular._
We want you on this journey with us, just as you’ve been all along. 
Your support has made everything possible, and we know it will continue to shape the future of FluentBoards.
Thank you, once again. 
Wishing you a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year in advance!
