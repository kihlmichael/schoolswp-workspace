Hold onto your boards, because FluentBoards just got a serious upgrade! 
First of all, a big thank you from the bottom of our hearts! At the end of the day, you have been guiding FluentBoards’ development since day one. 
And just like every other update, we’ve listened to your requests this time, too!
You’ve been asking for features like **REST API Documentation** and advanced integration options with **outgoing webhooks** for quite a while. 
The wait is finally over! 
After working tirelessly, our team is thrilled to deliver them at last. On top of that, we’ve also introduced a **brand-new project view** : Table View. Besides, as always, this release is packed with impactful features, smart improvements, and essential bug fixes too.
In short, this update is all about more flexibility, more customization, and more control.
Let’s dive in and explore!
## Table View in Task
FluentBoards is all about flexibility, and we’re taking that flexibility a step further!
![FluentBoards project view option : Table View](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-10.22.26-AM-1024x540.webp)
Until now, you had three project view options to choose from—Kanban, Calendar, and List. 
Today, we’re super excited to introduce yet another view option: **Table View**.
With Table View, you can see your tasks in a clean, spreadsheet-like format. This helps you plan and manage projects more effectively, making it easier to sort, scan, and organize information at a glance.
**How it helps:**
  * Get a compact overview of all your tasks at a glance
  * Perfect for users who love tabular layouts
  * Sort, filter, and bulk-update tasks in seconds
  * Track multiple task attributes side by side like a pro

In short, Table View brings more clarity, speed, and flexibility to [the way you organize projects](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/how-to-organize-a-project/>).
## Outgoing Webhook
With this update, FluentBoards enters the world of endless possibilities!
Yes, say hello to seamless automation!
![FluentBoards Outgoing Webhook](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-10.17.48-AM-1024x347.webp)
FluentBoards now supports **Outgoing Webhooks**. 
This will allow FluentBoards to automatically send data to a specified URL whenever certain events occur—such as when a task is created, updated, or completed. 
This means you can seamlessly push updates from FluentBoards without manual effort and connect your boards and tasks with any or all of your favorite apps (Slack, Discord, Zapier, etc.) instantly.
**How it helps:**
  * Automate workflows with real-time task updates
  * Keep your team instantly informed
  * Eliminate manual updates and reduce miscommunication
  * Connect FluentBoards with virtually any tool

So, from now on, integrations are easier, and the door to limitless possibilities is wide open!
## REST API Documentation
For developers and advanced users, we’ve got great news! 
[FluentBoards REST API documentation](<https://developers.fluentboards.com/rest-api/>) is now available!
This documentation provides everything you need to interact programmatically with boards, tasks, users, and more. 
So, from now on, you can easily connect FluentBoards with your own apps, build custom workflows, and extend functionality exactly the way your team needs. 
**How it helps:**
  * Take full control over FluentBoards data
  * Build custom integrations tailored to your business
  * Create, update, or fetch tasks and boards from external systems
  * Perfect for agencies, SaaS teams, or developers building custom dashboards

## Task and Subtask Reminder
Here comes yet another killer feature! 
FluentBoards email notifications are now smarter and more powerful with Task and Subtask Reminders.
![FluentBoards Reminder for Tasks & Subtasks](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-4.41.44-PM-1024x718.webp)
From now on, whenever you set a due date for a task or subtask, you’ll have the option to add a reminder. 
This means you can choose to receive notifications exactly when you need them—30 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, or even 1 week before the due date.
So, simply set reminders for any task, choose when you want to be notified, and never miss a beat again! 
**How it helps:**
  * Stay ahead of deadlines
  * Reduce stress and last-minute chaos
  * Plan your day and week with confidence
  * Keep your projects running smoothly

## Bug fixes and improvements
The journey of fine-tuning continues! While this update puts the spotlight on your most-awaited features, we haven’t ignored routine bug fixes and have also introduced impactful improvements to make your workflow smoother.
  * **Improvement:** ‘Assigned’ & ‘Mentioned’ tab in my tasks section
  * **Improvement:** Added “Create from template” option in task create action in CRM Automation
  * **Improvement** : Improved member search to support partial name matches
  * **Improvement** : Additional translations
  * **Fixed** : Security Issues in board member invitations
  * **Fixed** : Roles Issues in board member management
  * **Fixed** : Frontend portal task creation issue in List view
  * **Fixed** : Incoming webhook task creation issue with watchers and members
  * **Fixed** : Email sent issue when self-assigned to task
  * Other Improvements & Bug Fixes

## Many more to come…
From day one, FluentBoards has aimed to be simple, limitless, and grow as your business grows. 
With this update, we’re a big step closer to that vision. We hope you’ll enjoy these little upgrades and find your workflow simpler, smoother, and faster. 
And don’t worry! We’re not stopping here! 
Our dev team is more determined than ever to deliver powerful, effective features, and **we want you along for the ride**. If you have any feature requests, don’t forget to [add them here](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>). 
Thank you once again for being the most vital part of the FluentBoards journey. 
Let’s keep shaping the future of project management, together!
