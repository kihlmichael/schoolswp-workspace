## Recurring Task
Drumroll! Here we go!
![Meme: FluentBoards Users Waiting](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/unnamed-18.webp)
Yes, it’s Recurring Tasks—one of the most upvoted features on our roadmap—and we’re thrilled to announce that we’ve finally made it happen!
From now on, project management will be simpler, more hassle-free, and less time-consuming. Whether it’s a regular weekly meeting or a task that repeats after a specific period, there’s no need to open separate tasks manually.
Just enable the recurring option, and everything runs on autopilot!
To enable this option, open the task you want to set as recurring, then scroll down and select the ‘**Repeat Task** ’ option.
![To enable this option, open the task you want to set as recurring, then scroll down and select the ‘Repeat Task’ option.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-24-at-4.21.41 PM-1024x504.webp)
Set the necessary options—choose how often you want to repeat the task (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly), pick the time, and you’re good to go! Your task will now repeat exactly the way you want.
But before hitting the Set button, make sure to check the box above it based on your needs. If you don’t select the ‘**Create a task when repeating** ’ option, it won’t create a new task—it will just update the due date.
**Note:** This feature is still ‘**Experimental** ’, and we’re committed to shaping it around your needs. We encourage you to try it and share your feedback [in our community](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/community/fluent-boards/home>).
## Roadmap Report
Execution relies heavily on insights! Without tracking your current status, comparing it to where you should be, and adjusting accordingly, no project can achieve its final goal.
That’s why we’ve introduced detailed reporting for Roadmaps.
To access the report, simply open any of your Roadmap boards, go to the ‘About This Board’ and click ‘**View Roadmap Report** ‘!

## External Storage Provider (Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3)
‘**Host your data yourself** ’ is undoubtedly one of the prime selling points of FluentBoards! But what if you want to host your data elsewhere?
In fact, when you’re running multiple projects with large attachments, or if you want to preserve your server space, sometimes this feature becomes a must-have.
And the good news is, we’ve delivered it in this update.
To explore this feature, head to your settings dashboard and select ‘**Features & Modules**’. There, you’ll find a new option called ‘**Media Storage** ’!
![FluentBoards: External data hosting ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-3.47.09 PM-1024x555.webp)
Click the Settings button and choose where you want to store your media files.
![FluentBoards: External Data Hosting
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-3.55.00 PM-1024x419.webp)
Complete the setup, and your media will be stored on the platform of your choice from now on.
![External Data Hosting - FluentBoards](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-3.58.22 PM-1024x832.webp)
**Note:** Currently, we offer ‘**Cloudflare R2** ‘ and ‘**Amazon S3** ‘ as storage options. However, many more are in the pipeline, and we plan to deliver additional choices soon!

## Board Archive Feature
![FluentBoards Board delete option
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/unnamed-19-1024x556.webp)
You hit the ‘Yes’ button, and the board is gone forever!
While there were archive options for tasks and stages, there wasn’t one for boards—until now.
No worries! In this update, we’ve fixed that. Just like stages and tasks, you can now archive boards too.
![Just like stages and tasks, you can now archive boards too.
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-4.45.45 PM-edited.webp)
And you’ll find all your archived boards here.
![Get all your archive boards here
](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-4.22.58 PM-1024x449.webp)
## Modifiable Time Track Logs
FluentBoards allows you to manually log your time, so you can keep track of your contributions and see how much time you’ve invested in any project.
However, since time is entered manually, mistakes can happen. Unfortunately, there wasn’t a way to fix these errors—until now!
Well, not anymore! You can now edit the time you initially logged.
![Timelog Edit Option](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-4.26.04 PM-1024x461.webp)
## New Filter Option (Custom Field)
Making project management simple and easy is our top priority. We believe that better scannability leads to better accountability while keeping things simple.
That’s why we’re continuously enhancing our filter options to make them as dynamic as possible. With this update, we’re taking another step forward.
![New Filter Option \(Custom Field\)](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Screenshot-2024-09-19-at-4.28.45 PM-1024x980-1.webp)
Previously, you could filter tasks by labels, assignees, stages, and due dates. Now, you can filter using custom fields too!
## Dynamic Paste Feature
Copy any link—whether it’s an image, PDF, or anything else—and paste it into any task.
It will automatically be added as an attachment.
Pretty cool, right?
