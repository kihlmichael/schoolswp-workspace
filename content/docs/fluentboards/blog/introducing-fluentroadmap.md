## First Things First: What is a Public Roadmap?
You probably already have an idea of what a roadmap is, but let’s quickly recap before we dive into FluentRoadmap!
A roadmap is essentially a **visual tool that aligns everyone involved in a project** , helping teams and stakeholders move forward together toward a common goal.
Still a bit abstract?
Well, let’s make it concrete with a real-life example!
Let’s say you run a software business. Naturally, you have a detailed plan and strategy for where you want your software to be after a certain period of time. But does this solely depend on you?
Undoubtedly, it was your idea to build this product, and you developed it your way. But at the end of the day, it’s the users who use it, right?
That’s why keeping them aligned with your plans and listening to their needs is key to ensuring your software’s long-term success.
So what do you do now?
You share your roadmap publicly with your users so they can see what’s happening behind the scenes, which features are planned next, and what has already been delivered.
Plus, with a public roadmap, users can suggest specific features, and others can upvote ideas they find useful. Meanwhile, your team gets a clear picture of **what to prioritize next** based on real user feedback.

## How FluentRoadmap Works?
For better understanding, let’s get back to our old example: you run a software company! 
Wouldn’t it be great if you could bring your users on the same page too and let them design the software in their own way?
Well, with FluentRoadmap, that’s very much possible. 
Let’s see how!
Start by incorporating FluentRoadmap with FluentBoards. 
To do so, go to the ‘**Settings Dashboard** ’ and select the **‘Feature and Module’** option.
![](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfK03JVYCry7wac6kkVUjMEv9TnpkmMrDuxIKn-FKE8_8uBJU_XQuFDJB4RBQNMdDUtxnZtUdhqP42NR1ol01xvkCGKld2qG_NqY1DFcLOxeo9y1ojZVJvw0ojkOfTUhDh1GDTLugX1DFSGbgcQckhykUKB?key=Enh_8usBPcoeMXcMtv1ApA)
Now, scroll down a bit and select and install FluentRoadmap from the ‘**Recommended Plugins and Addons** ’ section. 
Once you have successfully installed ‘FluentRoadmap,’ it’ll automatically integrate with FluentBoards!
Now it’s time to create your first roadmap and configure the necessary settings. 
To do so, click on the ‘**Boards** ‘ option at the top of the scroll bar and then hit the ‘**Add Board** ‘ button!
![click on the 'Boards' option at the top of the scroll bar and then hit the 'Add Board' button!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-2.53.21 PM-1024x271.webp)
And here comes the tricky part! 
You will see a new checkbox named ‘**Type Roadmap** ‘ under the description box. Once you check the box, it will reveal four predefined stages in front of you that you can edit at any time.
![You will see a new checkbox named 'Type Roadmap' under the description box. Once you check the box, it will reveal four predefined stages in front of you that you can edit at any time.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-2.58.20 PM-1024x484.webp)
Now go ahead and give your board a name. Add a brief description and hit the save button!
And, your newly created roadmap will look like this!
![your newly created roadmap will look like this!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-3.09.14 PM-1024x236.webp)
Now you are ready to design your roadmap exactly the way you want!
But before you proceed, I want you to keep an important aspect in mind! 
FluentRoadmap lets you decide which stages to showcase publicly and which to keep private. If you look closely beside the name of the stages, you’ll see two types of tags: ‘**Public** ‘ and ‘**Admin Only** ‘.
To make any of these stages private, click on the ‘**Settings Icon** ’ beside the stage and select the ‘**Make This Stage Admin Only** ’ option.
![To make any of these stages private, click on the ‘Settings Icon’ beside the stage and select the ‘Make This Stage Admin Only’ option.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-3.19.33 PM-1024x449.webp)
Oh! We’re now all set to go!
It’s time to share this roadmap with your customers!
To do so, click on the **‘info icon** ‘ at the top or click on the ‘**three dots** ‘ on the right side of the dashboard.
![To share roadmap with your client click on the 'info icon' at the top or click on the 'three dots' on the right side of the dashboard.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-3.21.38 PM-1024x243.webp)
And a screen will appear like this! 
![Send email to the idea submitter, commenters, and, upvoters when the stage is changed](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-3.24.27 PM-1024x578.webp)
Decide which page you want to showcase this roadmap on. Copy the shortcode from here! 
You can only showcase the same roadmap on one page at a time
Besides, if you want to send an email to the idea submitter, commenters, and, upvoters when the stage is changed, don’t forget to tick the checkbox!
Now go and paste the code on the intended page! And it will look like this!
![Once you paste the code on the intended page, it will look like this!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-4.01.02 PM-1024x344.webp)
So, your clients can now easily add their feature requests. 
They can also add comments and upvote specific features to increase their priority!
![They can also add comment and upvote specific features to increase their priority!](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-4.02.51 PM-1024x371.webp)
And no need to mention, that once anyone adds a feature request, it will automatically be added to your roadmap board.
![once anyone adds a feature request, it will automatically be added to your roadmap board.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-4.04.55 PM-1024x304.webp)
Additionally, you’ll be able to set priorities for features based on filters like the number of upvotes or comments!
![screenshot 2024 07 26 at 4.07.37 pm](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-26-at-4.07.37 PM-1024x340.webp)
See? 
You, your team members, and your clients are all on the same page, making clarity, transparency, and accountability a breeze!
