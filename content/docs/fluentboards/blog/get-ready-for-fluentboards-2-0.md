Hi there! Hold your boards tight!
Your favorite project management tool, FluentBoards, is about to get smarter, faster, and more impactful.
In 2025, we built the foundation.
We listened, learned, and made sure FluentBoards was ready to grow with teams doing real work, every single day.
Now, it’s time to accelerate.
We promised you 2026 would be different, and the journey begins with **FluentBoards 2.0**. This is **our biggest update yet** , and every feature in this release is shaped directly by your feedback. 
To learn more, let’s dive in and explore what this teaser post reveals about FluentBoards 2.0.
## First Things First: Why FluentBoards 2.0?
As teams grow, managing work becomes more than just checking off tasks!
What starts with a few simple boards often grows into overlapping projects, recurring workflows, and complex timelines. Over time, keeping everything organized begins to feel like juggling. What once felt easy can slowly start to slow teams down.
We’ve seen this pattern again and again across our users.
Teams rebuild the same boards, manually set up recurring projects, struggle to get a clear view of progress across timelines, and face unnecessary friction when collaborating or migrating from other tools.
That’s the challenge we set out to solve with FluentBoards 2.0.
Our goal with 2.0 is to deliver the most requested capabilities while keeping the experience clean, familiar, and easy to use, just like it has always been.
With this release, FluentBoards is designed to become more powerful, save significant time in daily operations, and remove friction from workflows that used to require manual effort.
However, the real challenge was making sure FluentBoards stays simple!
And we’re happy to say that we’ve stayed true to that mission.
## So, What to Expect from FluentBoards 2.0?
![FLUENTBOARDS 2.0 coming with the two most upvoted features that have been in the planning stage for a long time. ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Screenshot-2026-01-30-at-12.28.00-PM-1024x366.webp)
Okay, no more small talk. Let’s get straight to the point.
FluentBoards 2.0 is the most feature-rich update we’ve ever shipped. It’s shaped directly by your feedback, your feature requests, and real-world use cases from teams like yours.
### Our top five picks: Features you’ve been waiting for!
FluentBoards 2.0 includes several of the most upvoted features from our [public roadmap](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>), along with improvements actively discussed [in our community](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/space/fluent-boards/home>).
#### 1\. Gantt Chart View: Plan work with time in mind
As projects grow, knowing when things happen becomes just as important as knowing what needs to be done.
FluentBoards already offers multiple ways to manage work, including Kanban, List, Table, and Calendar views. These views are great for tracking tasks and progress. But many teams told us they also need a clearer way to understand timelines and long-term plans.
That’s where the Gantt Chart View comes in.
![Gantt Chart View comes in FluentBoards 2.0.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-02-06-11.02.02-1-1024x559.webp)
With the Gantt Chart View, you’ll be able to see how those tasks stretch across a timeline, where they overlap, and how they depend on each other.
![2026 02 06 11.03.32](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-02-06-11.03.32-1024x406.webp)Note: This is the initial version; the final design may change!
#### 2\. Pre-built board templates to start strong
One of the most requested features from our users has been pre-built board templates.
Until now, every new project had to start from scratch. Many of you shared how helpful it would be to have ready-made templates to kick off projects faster and with more confidence.
This is especially valuable for new users getting started with FluentBoards, and for teams looking for inspiration based on proven project structures.
![Pre-Built Board Templates for FluentBoards](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/photo_2026-02-06-10.46.36-1024x424.webp)Note: This is the initial version; the final design may change!
And, with FluentBoards 2.0, this is finally becoming a reality.
At launch, you can expect five pre-built templates designed to cover common project management needs. Based on your feedback, we will continue expanding this template library with future releases.
![You will get a dedicated dashboard to view, manage, and organize both pre-built and custom templates](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/photo_2026-02-06-10.46.40-1024x678.webp)Note: This is the initial version; the final design may change!
Alongside templates, FluentBoards 2.0 will also introduce meaningful UX improvements. You will get a dedicated dashboard to view, manage, and organize both pre-built and custom templates, making it easier to reuse what already works.
#### 3\. Automated board creation from templates
Here comes another highly cherished feature that has been in the works for a long time and is finally taking shape.
Yes, the bond between [FluentCRM and FluentBoards](<https://fluentboards.com/blog/how-fluentcrm-complement-fluentboards/>) is about to get even stronger.
![Right now, FluentCRM offers a single dedicated action for FluentBoards: Create Task. ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Screenshot-2026-02-06-at-10.18.31-AM-1024x389.webp)
Right now, FluentCRM offers a single dedicated action for FluentBoards: Create Task. 
With FluentBoards 2.0, this integration is set to become far more powerful.
![With FluentBoards 2.0, you will be able to automatically create a full project board from a selected template using FluentCRM automations. ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/photo_2026-02-06-10.46.44-1024x239.webp)
After the release, you will be able to automatically create a full project board from a selected template using FluentCRM automations. 
![screenshot 2026 02 06 at 10.49.26 am](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Screenshot-2026-02-06-at-10.49.26-AM-1024x858.webp)
These automations can be triggered by real-world events like a new order being placed, an order status changing, or a form submission being received.
#### 4\. Anyone can create boards, not just admins!
Previously, only admins could create new project boards. If other team members wanted to start a project, they had to request an admin to create it for them, adding unnecessary friction and delays.
With FluentBoards 2.0, that limitation is gone.
Now, any team member with the appropriate permissions can create their own boards. This empowers teams to move faster, reduces bottlenecks, and encourages more autonomous project ownership across your organization.
#### 5\. Improved Trello import via API
Already using Trello and considering a switch? FluentBoards 2.0 makes migration easier than ever.
Previously, moving your boards from Trello required a manual export and import process. It worked, but it was tedious and time-consuming, especially for teams with multiple boards.
With this update, you will be able to import your Trello boards directly using the Trello API.
Simply connect your Trello account, select the boards you want to migrate, and FluentBoards handles the rest. Your tasks, stages, and structure come over intact, no manual file handling required.
### And more!
Beyond the headline features, FluentBoards 2.0 will also include several other improvements that many of you have requested:
  * **Pinned Tasks** : Keep your most important tasks visible and accessible at the top of your boards
  * **Bulk Move Tasks to Board** : Move multiple tasks between boards in a single action
  * **Customizable Business Logo** : Add your own branding to the frontend experience

And, as with all our releases, you can also expect important bug fixes and performance improvements to make FluentBoards more reliable and enjoyable to use.
## Get Ready for a More Capable FluentBoards
Yes, that’s a wrap… almost.
We can’t wait to bring FluentBoards 2.0 to you and see how it will make your day-to-day workflow faster, smoother, and more efficient.
Our team is putting the final touches and refining a few last details, so very soon you’ll have a version of FluentBoards that is more capable, more powerful, and more impactful.
Until then, good luck with your projects!
