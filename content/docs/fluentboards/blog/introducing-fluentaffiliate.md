## FluentAffiliate at a Glance: The Key Features It Brings to the Table
FluentAffiliate has all the features you need to manage your affiliate program. And the good thing is, it’s not just built with admin needs on mind, rather affiliates’ convenience has also been taken into account. 
To make it easier for you to understand, let’s divide all FluentAffiliate features into two main categories:
### Admin Features: Set Up, Launch, and Manage Your Affiliate Program Effortlessly
![FluentAffiliate Admin Features: Set Up, Launch, and Manage Your Affiliate Program Effortlessly](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/9-1024x576.webp)
With FluentAffiliate, you can quickly set up, launch, and manage your affiliate program while keeping everything organized in one place.
  * **Built-in registration form** : With FluentAffiliate, you get a built-in affiliate registration form that lets you add affiliates quickly and easily
  * **All your information in one dashboard:** To give you full control over your affiliate program, FluentAffiliate offers a clean, easy-to-navigate dashboard where you can view and track all the important numbers.
  * **Option to group your affiliates:** FluentAffiliate lets you organize affiliates into custom groups for easier management and more targeted strategies.
  * **Full control over your commission structure:** With FluentAffiliate, you can choose how to pay your affiliates, set a fixed amount, a percentage, or create custom rules based on products or groups.
  * **Multi-domain support:** Running multiple websites? FluentAffiliate makes it easy to manage affiliates and track referrals across all your domains in one place.
  * **Personalized email notifications:** Ensure your affiliates stay updated with tailored automated emails for sign-ups, commission approvals, payouts, and more.
  * **Affiliate report export:** Easily generate CSV reports of affiliate performance, commissions, and payouts for tracking and analysis.
  * **One-click data migration:** Transfer affiliates, commission data, and past performance to FluentAffiliate with a single click, ensuring a seamless transition.
  * **Real-time affiliate performance tracking:** FluentAffiliate lets you track clicks, conversions, and commissions instantly so that you always know what’s working and who your top performers are.
  * **Hassle-free payout options:** FluentAffiliate makes it easy to pay your affiliates automatically through PayPal or manually, whichever works best for you. 
  * **Affiliate conversion script:** Place the shortcode on your thank-you page to track purchases or signups, ensuring conversions are recorded and commissions are attributed to the right affiliate.

### Affiliate Features: Let Your Affiliates Focus on Results, Not Processes
![Affiliate Features: Let Your Affiliates Focus on Results, Not Processes](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FluentAffiliate-1-1024x576.webp)
FluentAffiliate provides affiliates with their personalized dashboard to track their performance and manage activities.
  * **Easy access to important data:** Affiliates can view their referral links, monitor commissions, and stay updated on all key metrics directly from their dashboard.
  * **Built-in link generator:** Affiliates can easily promote your products using the built-in Affiliate Links Generator.
  * **Real-time referral tracking:** Affiliates can track clicks, conversions, and sales from their unique referral links, keeping a close eye on their performance.
  * **Multi-domain Links:** With FluentAffiliate, affiliates can create unique referral links for each domain they promote, allowing them to track and earn commissions from all their marketing activities across different sites.
  * **Personalization options:** Affiliates can easily update their payment email, adjust notification settings, and customize their dashboard to fit their preferences.
  * **Social sharing option:** Affiliates can easily share their referral links directly to top social media platforms right from their dashboard
  * **Custom affiliate landing pages:** Assign branded, personalized pages to affiliates for cleaner links, higher trust, and better conversions, all while tracking clicks, sales, and commissions seamlessly.
