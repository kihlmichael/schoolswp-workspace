Hello, good people! Shahjahan Jewel here, back with another exciting update from FluentBoards!
We’ve been listening closely to your feedback, and today, we’re thrilled to roll out FluentBoards version 1.48, a release packed with small but impactful enhancements.
From giving you more control over your My Tasks dashboard to introducing highly requested features like multi-select custom fields and task status filters, this update is all about making project management more personal, flexible, and efficient.
We’ve also fixed several bugs and polished the interface to bring you a smoother and more stable experience.
So, let’s dive right into the details!
## Customizable Tabs in ‘My Tasks’ of Dashboard
This one’s a game-changer! You can now customize your **My Tasks** Dashboard. 
![You can now customize your My Tasks Dashboard. ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-18-1024x243.webp)
Want to prioritize tasks in a specific order? Or hide the ones that don’t need your attention? 
From now on, you’ll see a new option called ‘Configure Tabs’ on your My Tasks dashboard. Just click and select which task types (Upcoming, Completed, Overdue, etc.) you want to display and in what order.
![unnamed \(19\)](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-19-1024x538.webp)
Hurrah! You now have full control over how your tasks are organized.
![Hurrah! You now have full control over how your tasks are organized.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-20-1024x234.webp)
Have any feature requests for the**My Tasks** dashboard or other features? Share them on [our roadmap](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/8/>)!
## Time Logs Now Offer Date Selection Options
Logging time just got more precise!
You can now select a specific date when logging time for any task.
![Time Logs Now Offer Date Selection Options](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-21-1024x576.webp)
This makes tracking your project’s timeline even more accurate and organized, giving you better insights into how and when time is being spent across your projects.
## Multi-Select Added in Custom Fields
A highly requested feature—now live!
Introducing multi-select custom fields. 
![Multi-Select Added in Custom Fields](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-22-1024x520.webp)
Add multiple options to a single field and capture more context with ease. Whether it’s tags, priorities, or categories, you can now choose as many values as needed to better categorize and manage your tasks.
## Filter by Task Status
Finding tasks just got easier!
Previously, you could filter tasks based on assignees, stages, due dates, priorities, labels, and custom fields. Now, we’ve taken it a step further by adding **task status** to the filtering options.
![we’ve taken it a step further by adding task status to the filtering options.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-23-1024x612.webp)
You can now filter tasks by whether they’re **Completed** or **Closed** , helping you focus on what matters most.
## Task Descriptions Now Support “Large Mode” View
Reading long task descriptions shouldn’t be a struggle. And with this update, you can now expand task descriptions into large mode. 
![unnamed \(24\)](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-24-1024x282.webp)
This helps when reading lengthy descriptions or adding more detailed information without squinting at the screen.
![This helps when reading lengthy descriptions or adding more detailed information without squinting at the screen.](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/unnamed-25-1024x634.webp)
## Other Improvements & Bug Fixes
We’re continuously working to improve FluentBoards, and this update comes with various other minor improvements and bug fixes aimed at enhancing your user experience. 
These aren’t entirely new features, but they’re impactful upgrades that will make your project management experience smoother. And we think you’ll love them!
Here is what improved and fixed: 
  * **Security:** Updated framework library
  * **Improvement:** Board duplication includes background and template
  * **Improvement:** Delete a time log
  * **Improvement:** Copy task links directly from the task card
  * **Improvement:** Navbar UI updated for small screens
  * **Improvement:** Task attachments now support uploading multiple files at once
  * **Improvement:** Added the ability to delete a time log
  * **Fixed:** Custom Login URL Redirect Issue in Roadmap Authentication Settings
  * **Fixed:** Task loading error in the CRM Contact Section when time tracking is disabled
  * **Fixed:** Issue where multiple clicks created duplicate boards
  * **Fixed:** Stages not appearing issue in global task add button
  * **Fixed:** Improved board and roadmap movement issues
  * **Fixed:** Media attachment functionality issue in task description drawers

## Performance, Control, and Consistency!
That’s all for this release!
Yes, this might be a smaller update compared to our previous ones, but we believe its impact is meaningful. We hope the new features and improvements introduced in this version make your project management experience even smoother.
However, our mission to deliver the most dynamic yet easy-to-use project management plugin is going full steam ahead. And as always, we need you with us on this journey.
[Keep sharing your thoughts](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/road-maps/fluent-boards/#/7/>), and let’s continue building a tool that works perfectly for you.
Thank you once again for choosing FluentBoards.
And, until I bring you the next update, wishing you a very happy FluentBoarding!
