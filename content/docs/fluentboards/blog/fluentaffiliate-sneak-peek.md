## What is FluentAffiliate?
[FluentAffiliate](<https://fluentaffiliate.com/>) is a powerful yet refreshingly simple self-hosted WordPress affiliate management plugin that empowers businesses of all sizes to launch, manage, and grow their affiliate programs with ease.
It’s built to make affiliate management smoother than ever by bringing everything you and your affiliates need into one intuitive dashboard. (**Yes, everything**!)
And the best part?
While delivering all the core features right at your fingertips, it never compromises on what matters most:**speed, performance, security, and effortless usability**.

## What You Can Expect?
FluentAffiliate has all the features you need to manage your affiliate program. To make it easier, we can divide the features into the following categories:
### Admin features
FluentAffiliate makes managing your affiliate program simple and stress-free. With a built-in registration form and a clean, all-in-one dashboard, everything you need is right at your fingertips. You can group your affiliates for better organization, customize your commission structure with ease, track performance in real time, and handle payouts without the usual headaches.
### Affiliate features
FluentAffiliate keeps the needs of affiliates in mind too! Each affiliate gets a personalized dashboard to track their performance and manage their activities effortlessly. They have easy access to important data, a built-in link generator, and real-time referral tracking. The dashboard also supports multi-domain links and offers personalization options to enhance their overall experience.
### Integrations
FluentAffiliate works smoothly with the popular tools ( LMS, CRM, Forms, etc.) you already use to run your WordPress business. These built-in integrations ensure your affiliate program runs without issues. Besides, they leave the door open for even more powerful extensions in the future.
