## Key Features of FluentCart
FluentCart packs everything you need to run your store seamlessly, from built-in coupons and lightning-fast checkout to subscription and license management, REST API support, and a dashboard full of powerful, real-time insights!
Here’s what it brings to the table: 
### Effortlessly handle any type of product
![Effortlessly handle any type of product](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Admin-Dashboard-product-lists-1024x636.webp)
FluentCart gives you the power to sell literally anything, be it a digital, physical, or hybrid product from the same store. And to make sure you do this effortlessly, FluentCart offers:
To make things even easier, FluentCart offers:
  * **Product Table:** Browse and manage all products in a clean, sortable table and find what you need instantly

  * **Advanced Filters:** Use visual conditional logic to layer filters and locate products in seconds

  * **Variations:** Display every size, color, or style with individual images to boost conversions

  * **Grouped Variations:** Organize subscription or payment-term options into clear, user-friendly groups

  * **Custom Slugs:** Create SEO-friendly, memorable URLs for every product

  * **Secure Download Links:** Deliver license-controlled digital downloads safely and efficiently

  * **Installment Payments:** Let customers split payments into easy installments and increase sales potential

  * **Setup Fees:** Add one-time setup charges for subscriptions, great for premium onboarding experiences

  * **Categories & Types:** Keep your catalog organized with custom product categories and types

  * **Smart Inventory:** Automatically flag out-of-stock variations so you never oversell

  * **Upgrade Paths:** Allow customers to upgrade subscriptions effortlessly and boost long-term loyalty

  * **Fully Responsive Design:** Every block and page is mobile-ready, ensuring your store looks stunning on any device

  * **Endless Customization:** From colors to layouts, you can tweak and tailor every element just the way you like it

### Manage your store safely and seamlessly
![Manage your store safely and seamlessly](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Store-Management-2048x1018-1-1024x509.webp)
FluentCart empowers you to run, organize, and scale your store effortlessly, all while keeping your data secure and operations smooth.
  * **Intuitive Dashboard:** Monitor and manage every store activity from one clean, powerful, and easy-to-navigate dashboard

  * **Role-Based Access:** Add team members with specific permissions, whether it’s a manager, accountant, or marketer, and ensure everyone only accesses what they need.

  * **Store Information:** Set your store name, logo, and address once and use them dynamically across invoices, emails, and templates

  * **Secure AWS Storage:** Deliver large digital files, like software, videos, or resources, safely through AWS S3, without putting extra load on your site

  * **Global Currency Support:** Sell worldwide with flexible currency settings and custom price formats suited to your market

  * **Branded Templates:** Create professional, white-labeled invoices, packing slips, and delivery notes, fully customizable, no coding required

  * **Smart Email Alerts:** Stay updated 24/7 with instant email notifications for new orders, deliveries, and status updates

### Checkout & payments that convert
![Checkout & Payments That Convert](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Checkout_page-1-811x1024.webp)
FluentCart delivers a modern, high-converting checkout experience with multi-gateway support and one-click account creation, helping you cut cart abandonment and boost sales.
  * **Checkout Signup:** Shop now, sign up later! Customers can create an account in one click after completing their purchase.

  * **Multiple Gateways:** Give customers the freedom to pay their way with Stripe, PayPal, Paddle (Razorpay, Mollie, Square, Authorize.Net, Airwallex, Paystack, Paddle, and more coming soon)

  * **Cash on Delivery:** Capture more sales with COD support and get instant order reports for complete clarity

  * **Custom Gateway:** Easily integrate local or niche payment options using webhooks for a seamless checkout experience

  * **Credit Link via Email:** Send secure payment links directly to your customers via email

  * **Conditional Order Bump:** Instantly boost your average order value with smart, conditional order bumps at checkout

  * **Custom Receipt Template:** Design fully branded, dynamic receipts using shortcodes that automatically update per order

  * **Shipment Management:** Set up flexible shipping zones with custom rates for a smooth delivery process

  * **Order Heatmap:** Spot your busiest hours and top-performing dates instantly with visual heatmaps

  * **Order Count:** Track order frequency and volume in real time for better trend analysis

  * **Detailed Revenue:** Break down gross and net earnings by product or category for precise insights

  * **Refund Record:** Keep tabs on refund totals, amounts, and turnaround times to build lasting trust

  * **License Stats:** Stay informed with live counts of active, expired, and renewed licenses
