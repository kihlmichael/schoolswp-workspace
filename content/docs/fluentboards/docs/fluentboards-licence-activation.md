FluentBoards Pro offers a variety of advanced features to enhance your project management. To access these features, you need to activate the Pro version with a license key. You can purchase FluentBoards Pro from [WPManageNinja](<https://community.wpmanageninja.com/portal/>). 
Once you have the license key, simply activate it to unlock all the Pro functionalities.
## **Part 1: Downloading Your Plugin and License Key** #
First, we need to gather the files and the license key required to unlock your Pro features. Everything you need is securely stored in your [WPManageNinja account](<https://wpmanageninja.com/account/>).
#### **Step 1: Download the FluentBoards Pro File** #
  1. Open a new tab in your web browser and log in to your **WPManageNinja Dashboard** (also known as your “My Account” page).
  2. Look at the menu on the left side of the screen and click on **Downloads**.
  3. Here, you will see a list of all the products you have purchased. Find **FluentBoards Pro**.
  4. Click the **Download** button located on the right side of the row.
  5. A file named something like `fluent-boards-pro.zip` will save to your computer. 

**Important:** Do not unzip or extract this file! WordPress requires it to remain in the `.zip` format for installation.
![fluentboards download](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/fluentboards-download-scaled.webp)
#### **Step 2: Copy Your Unique License Key** #
While we are still in your WPManageNinja account, let’s grab your license key. This key acts as your proof of purchase to unlock the premium tools.
  1. On the same left-hand menu, click on the **Licenses** tab.
  2. Find your **FluentBoards Pro** purchase in the list.
  3. You will see a long string of letters and numbers labeled **License Key**.
  4. Simply click the small **Copy** button right next to it. Your key is now saved to your computer’s clipboard, ready to be pasted later.
  5. Click on the **View List** button to see the activate site lists against this license key.

![fluentboards download 1](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/fluentboards-download-1-scaled.webp)
### **Part 2: Installing and Activating on Your Website** #
Now that you have your `.zip` file and your license key, let’s head over to your WordPress website to put them to use.
> **Crucial Note Before You Begin:** FluentBoards Pro is an “add-on” to the free version. **Do not delete the free version of FluentBoards!** Both the free version and the Pro version must be installed and active on your site at the same time for the plugin to work.
#### **Step 3: Upload and Install the Pro Plugin** #
  1. Log in to your **WordPress Admin Dashboard**.
  2. On the left-hand menu, hover over **Plugins** and click on **Add New Plugin**.
  3. At the very top of the page, click the **Upload Plugin** button.
  4. Click **Choose File** and select the `fluent-boards-pro.zip` file you downloaded earlier.
  5. Click **Install Now**. WordPress will take a few seconds to unpack the file.
  6. Once the installation is complete, click the blue **Activate Plugin** button.

#### **Step 4: Verify Your License Key** #
The plugin is installed, but the Pro features are currently locked. Let’s use the key you copied in Step 2 to unlock them!
  1. In your WordPress dashboard menu, find **FluentBoards** and click on **Settings**.
  2. Look at the left sidebar within the FluentBoards settings menu and click on **License Management** (it is usually at the very bottom).
  3. Click inside the blank text box that says **License Key**.
  4. Right-click and select **Paste** (or press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V on your keyboard) to insert the key you copied from your WPManageNinja account.
  5. Click the blue **Verify License** button.

![fluentboards download 2](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/fluentboards-download-2-scaled.webp)
#### **Step 5: Success!** #
Once you click verify, the system will check your key in real-time. If everything is correct, the screen will update to show a success message: **“Your license key is valid and activated.”**
![fluentboards download 3](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/fluentboards-download-3-scaled.webp)
**Congratulations!** You have successfully upgraded to FluentBoards Pro. You can now explore all the advanced project management features right from your dashboard.
