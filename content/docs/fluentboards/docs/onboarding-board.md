# Onboarding Your First Board

Table of Contents 
  * Creating Your First Board
  * Customizing Stages
  * Adding Task 

When you first install FluentBoards, you’ll be guided through the onboarding process to set up your first board.
## **Creating Your First Board** #
To begin, you’ll need to create your initial board by providing a **Name** and **Description**. Once done, click on the **Next Step** button to proceed.
![onboarding of fluent boards ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Onboarding-of-Fluent-Boards--scaled.webp)
## **Customizing Stages** #
By default, FluentBoards provides three stages: **Open, In Progress,** and**Completed**. If you require additional stages, simply click on the **Add Stage** button. 
Conversely, you can delete any default stages by clicking on the **Delete** icon button next to them. Click on **Next** **Step** once you’ve customized your stages.
![onboarding board stage](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Onboarding-Board-Stage-scaled.webp)
## **Adding Task** #
Finally, if you wish to add tasks to your board, you can do so here. Simply give your task a title and click on the **Create Board & Add Task** button. 
Alternatively, if you prefer not to add tasks at this time, you can **Skip this Step**.
![onboarding board task ](https://fluentboards.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/OnBoarding-Board-Task--scaled.webp)
Following that, you’ll gain access to your boards, where you can organize various elements, including the association of **FluentCRM** contacts.
This simple onboarding process ensures that you can quickly set up your Boards and begin organizing your Tasks efficiently.
