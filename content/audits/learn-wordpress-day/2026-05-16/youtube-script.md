## Script complet (6'30 cible)

### [00:00 - 00:15] HOOK + PROMESSE

(Plan rapproché Michael face caméra, énergie posée)

**Michael** : "Yes. You can learn the basics of WordPress in a day. No, you will not be an expert by dinner time. I spent 8 hours timing exactly what is realistic when you start from zero, and in this video, I'll show you the honest result. Not the marketing version. The real one."

(Cut to: timer animation 8:00:00 démarre en bas d'écran, sera référencé tout au long)

### [00:15 - 00:45] SETUP DU CHALLENGE

(B-roll : laptop vide, notebook open, Michael s'assoit)

**Michael** : "Here is the rule I set for myself. 8 hours, one day, one full functional WordPress site. Starting from nothing: no hosting, no theme, no clue. Just a goal: by the end of the day, I want a real website online, that I can actually edit on my own. Not perfect. Functional."

"I'll also tell you exactly what I could not do in 8 hours, so you go in with the right expectations."

(Texte à l'écran : "RULE : 8 hours · From zero · One real site · No expert pretensions")

### [00:45 - 01:30] HOUR 1 - HOSTING + DOMAIN

(B-roll : capture browser sur hébergeur, sélection plan, paiement)

**Michael** : "Hour 1. I picked a host. I went with EasyHoster because that's the one I personally use for schoolsWP. The choice itself takes 10 minutes if you do it right: stay in your budget, pick something with one-click WordPress install, ignore the upsells. I bought my domain, ran the installer, and 45 minutes in, I had a fresh WordPress install."

(Texte à l'écran : "TIP : Don't spend 3 hours comparing hosts. Pick one in the top 5 reviewed by people who actually run WordPress sites.")

"That's the first time I logged into the WordPress dashboard. And honestly, it can be intimidating."

### [01:30 - 02:30] HOURS 2-3 - DASHBOARD TOUR

(B-roll : capture dashboard, sidebar tour, key menus)

**Michael** : "Hours 2 and 3. I gave myself a full hour just to look around the dashboard without doing anything. This was the most important learning of the day. Not building. Understanding the layout."

"Posts versus pages. The media library. The Appearance section. The Plugins section. The Users settings. If you skip this and try to build immediately, you will get lost. I have seen this mistake too many times in schoolsWP support requests."

(Capture animation : flèche pointant chaque menu sidebar, label rapide)

"By the end of hour 3, I could open the dashboard and find anything in under 10 seconds. That alone is half the battle when you learn WordPress in a day."

### [02:30 - 03:30] HOUR 4 - THEME SELECTION

(B-roll : capture theme installer, recherche Kadence, install, preview)

**Michael** : "Hour 4. Theme time. I picked Kadence. Free version. Block-native, lightweight, generous for beginners, and it does not lock you in. I installed it, activated it, and applied two colors and a font. That's it."

(Texte à l'écran : "Theme choice : Kadence (free). Block-native, fast, beginner-friendly.")

"This is where most beginners spend 6 hours testing 12 themes. Don't. Pick a theme that loads fast, has good reviews from real WordPress users, and stop there. You can change theme later. You cannot get back the 6 hours you wasted comparing fonts."

### [03:30 - 04:45] HOURS 5-6 - PAGES + POSTS

(B-roll : capture Gutenberg editor, création page Home, About, Contact, premier post)

**Michael** : "Hours 5 and 6. I built the actual content. Three pages: home, about, contact. One first blog post. That is what counts as a functional WordPress site in my book."

"The Gutenberg editor felt foreign in the first 20 minutes, then it clicked. Blocks are just containers. Text block, image block, button block. That's 80% of what you need on day one."

(Capture demo : ajout block paragraphe, block image, block bouton, sauvegarde)

"By the end of hour 6, I had a real homepage that did not look like a template demo. It was simple. But it was mine."

### [04:45 - 05:30] HOUR 7 - ESSENTIAL PLUGINS

(B-roll : capture plugin installer, 3 plugins, install Now)

**Michael** : "Hour 7. Plugins. This is where you can lose another 6 hours if you are not careful. I installed exactly three plugins. No more."

"One. Fluent Forms for the contact form. Two. SecuPress for basic security. Three. FlyingPress for cache and performance. Done."

(Texte à l'écran avec icônes : "1 Contact form · 2 Security · 3 Cache")

"You do not need 27 plugins on day one. Every plugin is a maintenance cost. Three solid ones beat fifteen mediocre ones, every single time."

### [05:30 - 06:15] HOUR 8 - FINAL REVIEW + GO LIVE

(B-roll : capture mobile preview, test contact form, click Publish, site live)

**Michael** : "Hour 8. Final review. I checked the site on mobile, sent a test contact form, fixed two typos, and clicked Publish. By 18:00, my site was live."

(Capture : timer compte à rebours arrive à 00:00:00, site live visible)

"Functional WordPress site. From zero. In 8 hours. Done."

### [06:15 - 07:00] HONEST VERDICT

(Plan rapproché Michael, ton plus posé)

**Michael** : "Now. The honest part. What did I NOT learn in 8 hours? Advanced SEO. I have a plugin installed. I do not understand SEO strategy yet. Performance optimization beyond cache. Custom code. Real security hardening. E-commerce with FluentCart or WooCommerce. None of that fits in one day."

"What I did learn? Autonomy on the essentials. I can manage the site on my own. I can publish posts. I can add pages. I can update plugins. I can recognize when something is breaking. That is a real starting point, and it is more than enough to launch."

### [07:00 - 07:45] WHAT TO DO NEXT

**Michael** : "If you want to try this for yourself, here is what I would do. Block one full day in your calendar. Use the 8-hour plan I just described. Get hosting, install WordPress, do the dashboard tour, pick one theme, build three pages and one post, install three plugins, go live."

"And here is the catch. Day 1 is not the finish line. It is the starting line. You will need practice, real practice on a real site, to get good. WordPress is a marathon, not a sprint. But starting strong on day 1 saves you months later."

### [07:45 - 08:00] CTA + OUTRO

**Michael** : "Two things if this was useful. First, link to the full written guide is in the description. Same 8-hour plan, more detail, every link you need."

"Second, I made a printable 8-hour WordPress checklist PDF. Free. Link in the description as well. Print it, follow it, send me your screenshots when your site goes live. I read everything."

"Subscribe if you want more honest WordPress tutorials, no marketing fluff, just what actually works for creators. See you in the next video."

(Card animée : article link + PDF link + subscribe button, 5 secondes hold)
