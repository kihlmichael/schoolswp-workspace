Now with these two variation options defined sure cart is going to create all the possible combinations for me so I'll have red small red medium red large green small and so on and so forth and for each of these combinations I can set a specific price a specific sku number and specific inventory levels so let me go ahead and fill in some of these details now I can set the price for each variation differently which is really useful if your large size costs more than your small size for example I can go to the large variations and set a higher price for those and then for inventory I can toggle on the inventory tracking for this product which will enable me to set a specific stock quantity for each variation so if I have 10 red smalls and 5 red mediums I can set that up here and once a variation sells out it will automatically show as out of stock on the product page and buyers won't be able to purchase it until you update the inventory levels now let me show you how this looks on the front end so I'll click on view product and here you can see the variation selectors for color and size and as a buyer I can select the color I want and the size I want and the price will update based on my selection and if I select a combination that is out of stock you'll see it's clearly indicated and the add to cart button is disabled so the buyer knows they can't purchase that particular combination right now this is all handled automatically by sure cart based on the inventory levels you've set.

Now let me also show you the inventory management side of things outside of variations if you have a product that doesn't have variations but you still want to track inventory you can do that as well so for any product you can scroll down to the inventory section and toggle on the inventory tracking and then set the current stock quantity so if I have 50 of this product in stock I'll set that to 50 and sure cart will automatically decrease this number each time a purchase is made and when it reaches zero the product will show as out of stock and buyers won't be able to purchase it now there's also an option for backorders if you want to allow buyers to purchase even when you're out of stock you can enable backorders which will still let the purchase go through but you'll need to fulfill the order once you have more stock available this is useful for products that you can easily restock so in summary product variations and inventory management in sure cart give you complete control over how your products are sold including the ability to offer different combinations of a product at different prices and to track and manage your inventory automatically to prevent overselling.
