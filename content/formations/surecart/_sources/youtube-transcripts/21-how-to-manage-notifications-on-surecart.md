# How to Manage Notifications on SureCart

sure card sends new notifications when there's new orders and other activities happen on your store and I'm going to show you how to disable these or set those to how you want you do this by logging into the platform at app.shurecard.com over here in the top left make sure you've selected the correct store if you have more than one store on your account down here at the bottom you'll see your name click on that and right here we'll click on notifications we have this panel that slides out and these are the notifications that are set to be sent to you any notification that you do not want to receive you can simply click on it and it will toggle It Off and then you can click on Save don't worry you can always change your mind later by doing the same I'll click on my name I'll click on notifications here for new orders I'll turn that back on and I'll click on Save and that's how you control the notifications that get sent to you from your store.
