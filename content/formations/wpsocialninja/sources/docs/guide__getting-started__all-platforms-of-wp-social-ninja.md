# Platforms | WP Social Ninja

## Platforms Overview [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#platforms-overview)

The **Platforms** page is the central hub for connecting all your social media and business accounts to WP Social Ninja. You must connect a platform here before you can create a template to display its content.

![The Platforms overview screen in WP Social Ninja](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/assets/WP-Social-Ninja-Platforms.NC2BUbTs.webp)

## How to Connect a Platform [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#how-to-connect-a-platform)

To connect an account, select the platform you want from the list and click **Connect**. A pop-up will appear asking you to log in and authorize the connection. Once approved, your account will be successfully linked.

## Platform Categories [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#platform-categories)

You can filter the list to find a specific type of platform by clicking on the tabs at the top of the page.

### Social Feeds [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#social-feeds)

This category includes all platforms for displaying live posts, photos, and videos on your website.

> Examples: [Twitter](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/twitter-configuration), [YouTube](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/youtube-configuration), [Instagram](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/instagram-configuration), and [Facebook Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/facebook-integration).

### Business Reviews [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#business-reviews)

This category is for fetching and displaying customer reviews from various business and e-commerce platforms.

> Examples: [Google Business Profile](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/google-configuration), [Yelp](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/yelp-configuration), [Facebook Reviews](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/facebook-configuration), [Airbnb](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/airbnb-configuration), **Amazon**, [WooCommerce](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/woocommerce-reviews), [Trustpilot](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/trustpilot-configuration), [Booking.com](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/booking-com-configuration), and more.

### Social Chat [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/all-platforms-of-wp-social-ninja#social-chat)

This category includes all the communication channels you can add to your website's Chat Widget.

> Examples: Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Slack, and 15+ other channels.

After you have successfully connected your account (which will display a "Connected" status), your next step is to navigate to the [Templates](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/templates-overview) menu. From there, you can create and customize a new template to display the feed, reviews, or chat widget on your site.
