# User Interface | WP Social Ninja

After you install WP Social Ninja, you will manage all your feeds, reviews, and chats from the main plugin dashboard. This guide explains the purpose of each section in the WordPress admin sidebar menu.

## Dark/Light Mode [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#dark-light-mode)

As shown in the image, WP Social Ninja includes a dark and light mode for the plugin dashboard. You can toggle this setting by clicking the sun/moon icon located in the top-right corner of the dashboard to switch to your preferred viewing mode.

## Dashboard [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#dashboard)

This is the main overview page for the plugin. It provides a quick summary of your templates and offers easy access to support and documentation.

## Platforms [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#platforms)

This is the most important step. Before you can create a template, you must connect your accounts in this section. Go here to add your [Google Business Profile](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/google-configuration), [Facebook Page](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/facebook-integration), [Instagram account](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/instagram-configuration), and any other available platform you want to display.

## Templates [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#templates)

This is the main area where you create and manage all your display [templates](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/templates-overview). A template is a saved layout and style that you can embed on your site using a [shortcode](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/integrations/shortcode-usage).

As shown in the image, this page is organized into tabs:

*   **Social Feeds:** All your templates for [Instagram](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/instagram-configuration), [Facebook](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/facebook-integration), [Twitter](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/twitter-configuration), [TikTok](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/tiktok-feed-configuration), etc.
*   **Business Reviews:** All your templates for [Google](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/google-configuration), [Yelp](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/yelp-configuration), [Facebook](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/facebook-configuration), etc.
*   **Chat Widgets:** Your templates for the [social chat widget](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-chat/chat-widget-configuration).
*   **Notification Popup:** Your templates for [review notification popups](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/advanced-features/notification-popup).
*   **Testimonials:** All your templates for [manually added testimonials](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/advanced-features/add-testimonials).

![The main Templates management screen in WP Social Ninja](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/assets/user-interface.B-VxH08M.webp)

## Quick Builder [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#quick-builder)

On the **WordPress Dashboard → Wp Social Ninja**, you will see a **Quick Builder** button. This button launches a simple, 3-step pop-up wizard to help you create a new template for reviews or feeds very quickly, without going through the full, advanced editor.

![The Quick Builder button on the dashboard](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/assets/quick-builder.OHAwuFen.webp)

## Testimonials [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#testimonials)

If you want to display a testimonial, go to this section to add and manage your [testimonials](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/advanced-features/add-testimonials). Once you've added them, navigate to **Templates → Testimonials** to create a custom template for displaying them on your site.

## Custom Source (Beta) [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#custom-source-beta)

This section allows you to create a custom feed by manually adding your own content, such as images, videos, and text. This is useful for creating unique testimonial displays or custom galleries that are not pulled from a social media platform.

## Reviews [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#reviews)

In this section, you'll find all the individual business reviews you've collected from connected platforms like [Google](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/google-configuration) or [Yelp](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/yelp-configuration). You can view, filter, and manage all your reviews in one organized list for easy access and control.

## Notification Popups [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#notification-popups)

This is a shortcut that takes you to the **Templates > Notification Popup** tab. You can create and manage your [pop-up templates](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/advanced-features/notification-popup) here.

This is a shortcut that takes you to the **Templates > Chat Widgets** tab. Go [chat widget](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-chat/chat-widget-configuration) to build and customize your website's social chat widget.

## Support [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#support)

This section gives you quick access to helpful resources, including detailed documentation, video tutorials, and direct links to our support team. So, you can get assistance whenever you need it.

## Settings [​](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/getting-started/getting-started-with-the-user-interface-of-wp-social-ninja#settings)

This is the global settings panel for the entire plugin. You can manage your License Key, set plugin-wide settings, configure platform APIs (like the Google API), and control cache settings (auto-sync frequency).
