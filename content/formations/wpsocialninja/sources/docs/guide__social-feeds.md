# Social Feeds | WP Social Ninja

**WP Social Ninja** has multiple **Social Feeds**, especially the most extensive and useful Feeds to offer.

Just like [Business Reviews](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/business-reviews/), **Social Feeds** can also fetch feeds from your social networking site within a few seconds. For instance, **Social Feeds** like [Instagram Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/instagram-configuration), [YouTube Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/youtube-configuration), [Facebook Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/facebook-integration), [Twitter Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/twitter-configuration), and [TikTok Feeds](https://docs.wpsocialninja.com/guide/social-feeds/tiktok-feed-configuration) will automatically enhance your business if you add them to your WordPress site.

**WP Social Ninja** has **Five Social Feeds currently:** Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok.
