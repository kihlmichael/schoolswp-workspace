How to structure a WordPress article to improve readability

01
Use headings
Divide your text with headings (H2, H3, etc.) to guide readers through your post.
02
Short paragraphs
Keep your paragraphs short, ideally 2–3 sentences, to facilitate reading on different devices.
03
Bulleted or numbered lists
Use lists to present information clearly and concisely.
04
Insert images and videos
Add visual elements between blocks of text to maintain interest and illustrate your points.
05
Use internal links
Connect your articles together to improve navigation and reinforce your site’s authority.
