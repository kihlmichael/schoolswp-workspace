[FluentCRM](https://fluentcrm.com/) allows you to create **Labels** to organize and filter your **[Automations](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/introduction-to-fluentcrm-automation/)**. This article will guide you through creating and managing the **Labels** for **Automations**.

## Creating Labels  [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/labels-for-automations\#creating-labels%C2%A0)

To learn how to create the **Labels** in **FluentCRM**, follow the steps with the screenshots below –

First, go to the **Automations** section from the **FluentCRM Navbar**, click the **Three-dot-icon** in the top right corner, and press the **Manage Labels** button.

![Manage labels button from three dot icon under automations](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/1.-Manage-Labels-button-from-Three-dot-icon-under-Automations-scaled.webp)

Click the **Create New Label** button to create new labels.

Here, you can also find all your existing labels. You can edit any labels by clicking the **Pencil Icon** or delete by clicking the **Trash Icon** at any time if needed.

![Create new label button edit & delete icons](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2.-Create-New-Label-button-Edit-Delete-icons-1-scaled.webp)

Now, provide a **Name**, select any desired **Color**, and click the **Create** button.

And, your **Label** will be created to help you categorize your Automations!

![3. create button](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/3.-Create-button.webp)

## Applying Labels [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/labels-for-automations\#applying-labels)

Return to the **Automations** section from the **FluentCRM** Navbar and select the **Automation/s** where you want to apply the Labels.

> You can select automations indvidually by clicking their **Checkboxes**, or select all at once using the **Checkbox** at the top left beside the **ID** column.

Once you select the desired Automation/s, you will get the **Select Action** box in the top left corner.

Now, click the **Dropdown Arrow** and select the **Apply Label** s action.

Then, choose the desired **Labels** (single or multiple according to your needs) from the **Select Labels** box by clicking the Dropdown Arrow.

Finally, click the **Apply** **Label** button and the labels will be applied.

![Apply label button](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/4.-Apply-Label-button-scaled.webp)

Here, you can see the applied labels have appeared with their chosen automations under the **Labels** column.

![Labels added to selcted automations](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/5.-Labels-added-to-selceted-automations-scaled.webp)

## Filtering Automations by Labels [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/labels-for-automations\#filtering-automations-by-labels)

You can also filter the list of automations by the applied labels.

To do this, click the **Sort Icon** and choose the desired label from the dropdown list.

![Filter by label dropdown](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/6.-Filter-By-Label-dropdown-scaled.webp)

For example, here I chose the **FluentCRM** **Label** from the list and only the **Automations** with the **FluentCRM** label appeared.

![All desired labelled automations](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/7.-All-desired-labelled-automations-scaled.webp)

## Deleting Labels [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/labels-for-automations\#deleting-labels)

If you want to delete any existing label/s, click the **Cross Icon** next to the **Label Name**, and press the **Yes** button.

This way you can remove the labels from any automations.

![Delete label](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/8.-Delete-Label-scaled.webp)
