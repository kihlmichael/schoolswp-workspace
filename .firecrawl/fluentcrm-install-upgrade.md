## Install and Activate FluentCRM [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license\#install-and-activate-fluentcrm)

You can install FluentCRM free version like any other WordPress plugin. Follow the steps given below –

- Go to the WordPress Admin **Dashboard → Plugins → Add New**
- Type in ‘ **FluentCRM**’ on the search box, click the **install** button and then **Activate** it.
- Another method to download the .zip file and upload this on the Add New page is by using the **Upload Plugin** button, after that click **Install**& **Activate**.

![fluent crm install](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Fluent-CRM-Install.png)

### Installing and Activating FluentCRM Pro [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license\#installing-and-activating-fluentcrm-pro)

To get FluentCRM Pro, purchase the plugin from [here](https://fluentcrm.com/pricing/), and you’ll be redirected to your WPManageNinja account. After a successful purchase, you can download the **Pro** version and get the **License Key** from your WPManageNinja [Account](https://wpmanageninja.com/account/dashboard/).

![fluentcrm licensekey](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FluentCRM-LicenseKey.png)

Go to your WordPress dashboard and navigate to **Plugins > Add New Plugin** from the left sidebar. Click on the **Upload Plugin** button, select the file, and upload it. Then, click **Install Now** and activate the plugin. It’s a simple process!

![fluentcrm upolad pro](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FluentCRM-Upolad-Pro.png)

## Update FluentCRM [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license\#update-fluentcrm)

It’s pretty easy to update both the Free and Pro versions. You can navigate to the plugin page of your dashboard, in order to check if an update is available. If any update is available just click on the update button.

Make sure that the pro version is activated with a valid license key. If you don’t have a license key or the license key is expired, you can purchase a new one or renew the existing one from **[here](https://fluentcrm.com/#pricing)**

## Activate FluentCRM License [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license\#activate-fluentcrm-license)

FluentCRM Pro comes with a licensing option to activate your license. Follow these simple steps to activate the license –

Go to **FluentCRM** and click on the **Settings** option from the sidebar. Then click the **License Management** tab, here you’ll find the option for providing the **License Key**. So, after adding the key just click the **Verify License** button. Now you have successfully activated your license!

![fluentcrm license](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FluentCRM-License.png)

## Renew FluentCRM License [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/how-to-install-upgrade-and-activate-license\#renew-fluentcrm-license)

FluentCRM Pro comes with a licensing option to renew your license. Follow these simple steps to renew the license:

You can start by going to the purchase history from the WPManageNinja [Account](https://wpmanageninja.com/account/dashboard/). Then you’ll see the ‘Renewing a license key?’ link on the checkout cart. Click on that.

![fluentcrm license renew 1](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FluentCRM-License-Renew-1.png)

After entering the license key, click the **Apply License Renewal** button then your license will be activated automatically.

![fluentcrm license renew 2](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FluentCRM-License-Renew-2.png)
