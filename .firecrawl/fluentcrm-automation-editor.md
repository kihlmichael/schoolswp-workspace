The most important use of the [FluentCRM](https://fluentcrm.com/) is to use the Automated Actions based on some triggers that happen on the WordPress Website. FluentCRM specializes in such automation triggering various incidents and then taking action upon user conditions and requirements.

This article will guide you through the process of creating new automations and the editor of FluentCRM Automation which lets you create an automation funnel, select the trigger, add actions & conditions the way you want to set up, and choose to end the automation. To learn the whole process, follow the article accordingly.

## Creating A New Automation [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/automation-editor\#creating-a-new-automation)

To learn how to create new automation in FluentCRM, follow the steps with screenshots below –

First, go to the **Automations** section from the **FluentCRM Navbar** and click on **\+ New Automation.**

![automations section from fluentcrm navbar](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Automations-section-from-FluentCRM-Navbar-scaled.webp)

Once you click the **\+ New** **Automation** button, a pop-up page will appear with a couple of popular **Pre-built Funnel Templates** to reduce your hassle of creating automation from scratch every time.

Now, you can choose any of the **Pre-built Funnel** **Templates** according to your needs.

Or, you can create your automation from scratch by simply clicking the **Create from Scratch** box (e.g., here, I am going to create a new automation from scratch).

![create from scratch option](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Create-From-Scratch-option-scaled.webp)

Now a pop-up will come up from where you can select the **Trigger** for your Automation that suits your needs and click the **Continue** button.

Also, you can give a **Title** to your Automation in the **Internal Label** field for easy referencing later. But, if you leave it blank, the title will be automatically set based on your chosen trigger.

> Apart from the triggers shown below, FluentCRM also comes with lots of other triggers. To see the full list of triggers, [click here](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/introduction-to-fluentcrm-automation/#triggers).

For example, I’ve selected the **List Applied** trigger under the **CRM** option. As a result, this automation funnel will trigger when the selected list will be applied to any of the **FluentCRM Contacts.**

![choose trigger for automation](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Choose-Trigger-for-automation-scaled.webp)

Once you click continue, another popup will appear, now fill in all the fields according to your requirements and click **Save Settings** once you are done.

![list applied trigger's pop up page](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/List-Applied-Triggers-pop-up-page.webp)

Once you choose the **Trigger**, you will be redirected to the similar screen below from where you can add the [Action](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/primary-automation-actions/) blocks, [Conditional Automation Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/conditional-automation-actions/), and [Goals or BenchMark Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/goals-or-benchmark-actions/) into your Funnel.

To add Actions, click the **Plus Icon** and press on the **Add Action / Goal** or **Conditional Action.** For example, I’ve selected the **Add Action / Goal** option for the automation.

![add action : goal option](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Add-action-goal-Option-scaled.webp)

Now, a popup will appear with a set of [Action Blocks](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/primary-automation-actions/) from where you can choose any type of action block.

There are various actions that can be added like –

1. [Primary Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/primary-automation-actions/),
2. [Goals BenchMark Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/goals-or-benchmark-actions/),
3. [WordPress Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/wordpress-automation-actions/),
4. [Email Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/automation-email-actions/),
5. [LMS Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/lms-automation-actions/), and
6. [Miscellaneous Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/miscellaneous-automation-actions/) along with the
7. [Conditional Automation Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/conditional-automation-actions/).

For example, here, I’ve selected **Apply Tag** as an Automation **Action**.

![choosing actions](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Choosing-Actions.webp)

Once you choose the action, another popup will appear, now fill in all the fields according to your requirements and click **Save Settings** once you are done.

If you don’t want to save click the **Trash Icon** to delete the action. Also, you can create new tags here by clicking the **Plus Icon**.

![applied tag action's pop up page](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Applied-Tag-actions-pop-up-page.webp)

Once you are done, **enable** the **Toggle** to **Publish** your automation so that your automation activates.

For example, when this automation runs, the contacts that are listed in the **Student** list will be tagged as **Enrolled**.

![publish the automation](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Publish-the-automation-scaled.webp)

## Automation Editor [\#](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/automation-editor\#automation-editor)

To learn how you can edit your existing Automations anytime, follow the steps with the screenshots below –

First, go to the **Automations** section from the **FluentCRM Navbar**, select the automation you want to edit, and click on its **Title.**

For example, here, I’ve selected the **Listed Applied** automation for customization.

![select automation you want to edit](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Select-automation-you-want-to-edit-scaled.webp)

Once you click the **Title** of your desired automation it will take you to the **Automation Editor** page with all the available options for your edit.

![All options of automation editor](https://fluentcrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/All-options-of-Automation-Editor-1-scaled.webp)

**All the edit options mentioned above are briefly explained below:**

1. **Settings Icon**: Click on this icon if you want to change the trigger for this automation. Once you change, please reconfigure the trigger settings.

2. **Stats Checkbox**: Check this box if you want to see all the **Statistics** of your automation funnel.

3. **Status Toggle**: Enable this option, to **Publish** and run your automation. Otherwise, your automation will be saved as a **Draft** only.

4. **View Reports**: By clicking this button, you can check the overall **Reports** of the Automations. To learn more detailed reporting details please visit [Automation Reports](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/automation-reports/).

5. **Guideline Resource**: This button will open a sidebar on the right-hand side with the documentation to guide you through the basics of FluentCRM Automation Actions.

6. **Existing Trigger**: If you want to edit any **Trigger** that you already added, just click on it and the edit options will open on the right-hand side.

7. **Existing Action**: If you want to edit any **Action** that you already added, just click on it and the edit options will open on the right-hand side.

8. **Three-dot-icon**: Once you click this icon, you will get two more options. These are:

- **Delete**: Click on this option if you want to delete the action.
- **Clone**: Click on this option if you want to clone/duplicate the action.

9. **Move Icon**: To move any action, please hover over the action you want to move and you will see **2 Arrows** beside them indicating up or below to move the action. Now, by clicking the **Arrows** you can easily move your action up and down within the funnel.

10. **Plus Icon**: Always pressthe **Plus Icon** to add new [Actions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/primary-automation-actions/) or [Conditions](https://fluentcrm.com/docs/conditional-automation-actions/).

This way you can create and edit automations easily at any time!
