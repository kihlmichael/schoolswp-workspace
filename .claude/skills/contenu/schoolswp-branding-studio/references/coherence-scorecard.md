---

## Axis Details

### 1) Tone (/20)

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Pédagogue, concret, no jargon
- Natural tutoyement
- Conversational without being informal
- Professional without being rigid

**Alignment signals:**
✅ Uses signature expressions ("En clair :", "Testé et approuvé")
✅ Action verbs ("fais", "évite", "teste")
✅ Questions to engage reader

**Deviation signals:**
⚠️ Academic or cold tone
⚠️ Aggressive marketing
⚠️ Excessive formality or unnecessary vouvoiement

---

### 2) Clarity (/20)

**Evaluation criteria:**
- 1 idea = 1 block
- Actionable sentences
- 8-15 words per sentence average
- 2-4 sentences per paragraph max

**Alignment signals:**
✅ Short, direct sentences
✅ Clear structure (intro-body-conclusion)
✅ Smooth transitions

**Deviation signals:**
⚠️ Sentences > 20 words
⚠️ Compact text blocks
⚠️ Multiple ideas in one paragraph

---

### 3) Mission/Promise Alignment (/20)

**Evaluation criteria:**
- WordPress + optimization (performance/SEO/automation) focus
- Autonomy-oriented
- "WordPress works for you" philosophy
- Actionable, not theoretical

**Alignment signals:**
✅ Step-by-step approach
✅ Concrete examples (FluentCRM, Elementor, WP Rocket, etc.)
✅ Reasonable promises based on proof

**Deviation signals:**
⚠️ Generic content applicable to any platform
⚠️ WordPress presented as complicated
⚠️ Promises without proof

---

### 4) Vocabulary (/20)

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Recommended words present (pas à pas, méthode, checklist, optimiser, performant, SEO, automatiser)
- Forbidden words absent (révolutionnaire, incroyable, garanti, 10x, secret, hack)
- Technical terms explained when used

**Alignment signals:**
✅ "méthode" instead of "secret"
✅ "efficace" instead of "révolutionnaire"
✅ "objectif" instead of "garanti"

**Deviation signals:**
⚠️ Hype vocabulary
⚠️ Jargon without explanation
⚠️ Empty marketing phrases

---

### 5) Structure (/20)

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Promise → proof/example → next action (simple CTA)
- Clear H2/H3 hierarchy
- Lists and formatting appropriate
- CTA present and natural

**Alignment signals:**
✅ Hook with concrete problem
✅ Examples or mini-checklists included
✅ Ends with simple action

**Deviation signals:**
⚠️ No clear CTA
⚠️ Missing examples/proof
⚠️ Structure unclear or missing

---

## Alignment Summary

### ✅ Aligned if:
- Step-by-step
- Concrete
- Reasonable promise
- No hype
- Optimization focus
- Clear structure
- Signature expressions used naturally

### ⚠️ Deviates if:
- Promises too strong
- Jargon without explanation
- Empty marketing
- Off-topic (not WordPress optimization)
- Generic content
- No actionable takeaway
