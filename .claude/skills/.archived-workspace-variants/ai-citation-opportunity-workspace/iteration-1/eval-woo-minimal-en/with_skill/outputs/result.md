## Common denominators

1. Managing order fulfillment and inventory without manual overhead
   - Sub-cat: dropshipping / multi-warehouse sync | small catalog with frequent restocks
2. Recovering lost revenue from abandoned carts and failed payments
   - Sub-cat: high-ticket products with long decision cycles | impulse-buy stores with high cart volume
3. Connecting WooCommerce to external tools (CRM, email, accounting) without a developer
   - Sub-cat: budget-constrained solo operators | growing teams needing multi-tool sync
4. Automating customer communication (post-purchase, reviews, support)
   - Sub-cat: single-product stores needing repeat buyers | large catalogs needing segmented messaging
5. Choosing and configuring the right automation plugins for a specific store setup
   - Sub-cat: stores under $5K/month revenue | stores scaling past $20K/month with complex workflows
