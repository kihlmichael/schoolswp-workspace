# Forms Block on Link Pages

> Source: https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/linkpages-forms-block/
> Updated on March 18, 2026

Forms are essential tools for collecting user input and data on a website. They serve various purposes, such as contact forms, registration forms, surveys, feedback forms, and more. On interactive websites, especially WordPress sites, forms are crucial for enhancing user engagement, collecting data, and facilitating communication.

ClickWhale has introduced a new feature that allows users to easily embed forms from popular plugins.

## How to Place Forms on Link Pages

Navigate to ClickWhale > Link Pages. Add a [new link page](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/creating-your-first-link-page/) or edit the existing one.

Go to the **Contents** tab.

Then, drag and drop the Forms block into the content area box on the right.

Now, choose a form plugin installed on your WordPress website and ensure that a form is created.

In the example, we used a forms plugin named Contact Form 7.

This is what it looks like on the front end. You can optimize the page by updating the styles in the Styles tab or by adding custom CSS based on your UX/UI requirements.

## What WordPress Forms Plugins Does ClickWhale Support?

ClickWhale supports various form plugins, including popular ones like:

- Contact Form 7
- Fluent Forms
- Ninja Forms
- WP Forms

Check out [all forms integrations](https://clickwhale.pro/integrations/).
