# How-To Delete My Account

> Source: https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-delete-my-account/
> Updated on July 27, 2024

If you no longer wish to use your ClickWhale Pro account and want to delete it, you can easily do so via the [Freemius dashboard](https://users.freemius.com/).

Go to the [Freemius login page](https://users.freemius.com/) and log in to your account. Once logged in, navigate to the sidebar menu and click on **My Profile**. Scroll to the bottom of the My Profile page and click on the **Close Account** button.

## Important Reminder

Before proceeding with account deletion, make sure to cancel all active subscriptions. This step is essential to prevent any unexpected charges or disruptions.
