# How-To Cancel your Subscription (Auto-renewal)

> Source: https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-cancel-your-subscription-auto-renewal/
> Updated on July 27, 2024

In this article, we will provide an easy guide on how to cancel your auto-renewal subscription for ClickWhale pro.

If you purchased a ClickWhale annual license, an auto-renewal is set by default. You will receive an email reminder a week before your upcoming payment.

If you'd prefer to cancel your subscription, start by logging into your [Freemius account](https://users.freemius.com/).

After you log in, click the **Renewals & Billing** in the sidebar menu. Then select your ClickWhale subscription, and your subscription information will appear on the right side of your screen, click the **Cancel Auto-Renew** link.

Remember that once your license expires, you will lose the following benefits:

**Price Lock:** The price at which you initially purchase ClickWhale Pro will remain the same, even if prices increase.

**Automatic Renewal**: You won't have to worry about forgetting to renew your license.

**Continuous Updates**: You will always have access to the latest updates.
