Installing FluentCRM is as easy as installing any other WordPress plugin. And the upgrading, activating license, renewing, etc. it just keeps on getting easier compared to other plugins. Let's see how.

From your WordPress Dashboard, choose Plugins from the left sidebar, and then click Add New. It will redirect you to the WordPress Plugin directory. Type FluentCRM in the search field. Here it is, just install it, and don't forget to activate it as well.

FluentCRM is now installed and you can locate it from the left sidebar of your dashboard. Let's set up your business and other basic information to work with FluentCRM. It's not that complicated, just follow a few simple steps.

First, provide your business information. Enter the name of your business, business address, and upload your company logo. This information will be used on your email footer as they are mandatory for sending marketing emails.

You may create the necessary lists to segment your contacts. Add a segment name. You can add multiple segments if you want. Simply click on the Add More to add more lists. No worries, you can do it afterward as well. Then press Next.

For filtering your contacts in a more targeted way, you will need tags in your contacts. Let's add some tags as well.

We are nearly done. If you want to create forms and collect email leads, you can also install Fluent Forms by keeping the checkbox enabled. Consider keeping the Share Essentials option and enter your email address to receive marketing advice and learn about advanced FluentCRM features. Now click on Complete Installation.

Our FluentCRM is ready to roll. Select GO to CRM Dashboard to get an overview. Here, you can monitor your contacts, tags, and other information. In the right sidebar, you may get the essential suggestions and some quick links.

If you want to use the FluentCRM Pro edition, click on Get Pro from the right corner or you can choose Upgrade to Pro Now. This will take you straight to the FluentCRM website, from where you can purchase it.

Once your done purchasing, you will receive a zip file and a license key. Let's look at the zip file installation process. Go back to the Plugins option. Click on Add New. The procedure is fairly simple. You are now using the Pro version of FluentCRM. But it needs to be activated with a valid license key. And that's pretty easy to do as well.

Only one more thing! FluentCRM will use the default mailing system of WordPress. If you're not using any SMTP service yet, we recommend using FluentSMTP.
