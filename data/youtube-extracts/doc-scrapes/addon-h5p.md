# Addon — H5P

> Source : https://docs.themeum.com/tutor-lms/addons/h5p/

## H5P

H5P is a powerful add-on that enables you to create interactive, engaging content like quizzes, videos, and simulations. By integrating H5P with Tutor LMS, you can enhance your online courses and provide a more dynamic learning experience for your students.

**Benefits of using H5P:**

- **Increased Engagement:** Interactive elements capture learners' attention and encourage active participation.
- **Improved Knowledge Retention:** Active learning with H5P activities helps students retain information more effectively.
- **Versatile Content Types:** H5P offers a wide range of content types to cater to diverse learning styles and preferences.

## Installing and Enabling the H5P Add-on

**Install the H5P Plugin:**

1. Go to "**Plugins > Add New**" in your WordPress dashboard.
2. Search for "**H5P**."
3. Click "**Install Now**" and then "**Activate**."

**Enable the H5P Add-on in Tutor LMS:**

1. Go to "**Tutor LMS Pro > Add-ons**."
2. Locate the "**H5P**" add-on and click "**Activate**."

## Creating H5P Content

**Access the H5P Content Creator:**

1. Navigate to **H5P Content** in your WordPress dashboard.
2. Click **Add New**.

**Select a Content-Type:**

1. Use the "**H5Phub**" dropdown menu to browse available H5P content types.
2. Alternatively, use the **Search** bar to find specific content.

**Download and Create:**

1. Click "**Get**" to download the chosen content type.
2. Once downloaded, click "**Details**" to open the content creator.
3. Click "**Use**" to start creating your H5P content.

**Add to Your Course:**

1. Once your H5P content is complete, click the "**Create**" button to save.
2. All H5P content will be readily available in "**WP dashboard > H5P Content > Libraries**"

### Leveraging H5P's Knowledge Base

H5P offers a range of resources to help you create interactive content. Each content creation tool includes "Tutorial" and "Example" buttons that provide specific guidance and tips tailored to the content type you're working on.

For comprehensive, step-by-step instructions on creating interactive content using H5P, you can also visit the official H5P documentation: https://h5p.org/documentation/for-authors/tutorials

## H5P Display Options

- **Display Toolbar Below Content:** Controls whether the toolbar is displayed above or below the content.
- **Allow Users to Download the Content:** Enables learners to download the content for offline access.
- **Display Embed Button:** Allows users to embed the content into their own websites.
- **Display Copyright Button:** Shows copyright information to protect your work.

## Using H5P Content in Tutor LMS

**Steps to use H5P content in Tutor LMS courses:**

1. **Navigate to the Course Builder** and open the **Curriculum** tab.
2. **Select a Topic** where you want to add H5P content.
3. Click on the "**Interactive Quiz**" button under the selected topic.
4. Enter a "**Title**" and optionally add a "**Summary**".
5. Click the "**+**" button to add H5P content from your library.
6. **Select** the desired H5P content and click "**Add**".

**Further Optimizations (Quiz Settings):**

**Basic Settings:**
- **Attempts Allowed:** Set the maximum number of times a learner can attempt the quiz.
- **Passing Grade:** Define the minimum score required to pass the quiz.

**Advanced Settings:**
- **Quiz Auto-Start:** Enable or disable automatic quiz start upon page load.
- **Question Order:** Choose between "Sequential," "Random," "Ascending and Descending" question order.

**Note:** Tutor LMS supports a wide range of H5P content types. However, some content types like **Game Map, Question Set, Interactive Book, Interactive Video, Course Presentation,** and **Personality Quiz**, aren't supported yet.
