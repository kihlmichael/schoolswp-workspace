> Source : https://docs.themeum.com/tutor-lms/tutor-lms-certificate-builder/templates/

## Templates

With the release of the Tutor LMS Certificate builder, you will get 25+ pre-built templates that have been built from scratch to meet every category of LMS types.

All the available templates are both in landscape and portrait orientation so you can choose the one that you are comfortable with.

Whether you are looking for a pre-built template to award your online kid's learning platform, or if you are looking for certificates that are appropriate for a corporate skill Tutor LMS certificate builder has it all.

Once you go through the templates creation process, you will be welcomed by a section prompting you to choose from an existing pre-built certificate template or start from scratch. You can also choose from categories ranging from the below list followed by the image.

- Professional
- Creatives
- Kids
- Achievement
- Minimal
- Musical
- University

And the best part of the pre-built templates is that they are all fully customizable. Meaning they have been made with the same elements, shapes that you can use to build your certificate from scratch.
