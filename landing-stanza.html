02. The Methodology

Complexity is the enemy of shipping.

01

Single Source of Truth

Data syncs instantly across bug reports, sprint boards, and executive roadmaps. Product and engineering no longer operate in siloed realities.

02

Keyboard-First Primitives

Navigate, create, assign, and update without touching the mouse. Built for developers who view context switching as an expensive transaction.

03

Signal over Noise

Automated triage rules and strict schema enforcement prevent backlog rot. Only actionable work makes it to the developer's queue.

Core Primitive

High-signal issue tracking.

Capture technical debt, feature requests, and bugs with structured precision. Custom schemas ensure every ticket has the necessary context before it reaches a sprint, eliminating the back-and-forth cycle.

  • Markdown everywhere. Full support for code blocks, tables, and latex.
  • Git integration. Auto-close issues when PRs are merged.
  • Custom workflows. Define states that match your actual shipping cycle.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, migration, and enterprise deployments.

How hard is it to migrate from Jira?
We offer a one-click importer that migrates your epics, issues, comments, and sprint history in minutes while maintaining relationships. We map your complex Jira workflows to Stanza's streamlined primitives.
Do you offer self-hosted or single-tenant options?
Yes. Our Enterprise tier includes options for VPC deployment via AWS Marketplace, ensuring strict compliance with SOC2 Type II and HIPAA requirements.
Is pricing based on seats or usage?
We use a flat predictable per-seat model for core contributors. Viewers and external stakeholders are always free, because collaboration shouldn't be taxed.
